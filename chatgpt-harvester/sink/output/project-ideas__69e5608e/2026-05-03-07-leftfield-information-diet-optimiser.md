## 7. Leftfield: “Information Diet Optimiser”


**Problem**  
People consume large volumes of low-quality information, degrading decision-making and focus.

**Approaches**
- Analyse content consumption patterns  
- Score information quality/value  
- Recommend optimised “information diet”  

**Tech hints**
- Content classification (topic, depth, novelty)  
- Browser history analysis (local-first)  
- Heuristic scoring models  

**Formats**
- Browser extension  
- CLI analyser  
- Weekly report tool  

**Why good fit**
- Strong modern relevance  
- Bridges tech and behavioural domains  

**Why not**
- Subjective quality metrics  
- Privacy concerns  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High

`information`, `focus`, `analysis`, `self-improvement`
