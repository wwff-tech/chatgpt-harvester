## 7. Leftfield: “Digital Twin for Personal Systems”


**Problem**  
People optimise parts of their lives (work, health, habits) in isolation, without modelling interactions.

**Approaches**
- Create a simplified “digital twin” of personal systems
- Simulate interactions between variables (sleep, work, focus)
- Explore optimisation scenarios

**Tech hints**
- System dynamics modelling (PySD)
- Data ingestion from trackers
- Scenario simulation

**Formats**
- Notebook-based tool
- CLI + visual output
- Web app

**Why good fit**
- Strong alignment with your thought-experiment approach
- Integrates multiple domains

**Why not**
- High modelling complexity
- Data quality challenges

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium

**Content potential**
- Very high

**Community potential**
- Medium

**Tags**
`simulation`, `systems-thinking`, `self-improvement`, `modelling`
