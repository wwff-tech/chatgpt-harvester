## 4. “Energy-Aware Focus Scheduler”


**Problem**  
Task scheduling ignores cognitive energy fluctuations, leading to poor performance.

**Approaches**
- Track energy levels (self-reported + inferred)  
- Align task types with energy states  
- Suggest optimal scheduling  

**Tech hints**
- Time-of-day modelling  
- Simple ML models for prediction  
- Local-first data storage  

**Formats**
- Desktop app  
- Calendar plugin  
- CLI + reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD/self-optimisation relevance  
- Practical and actionable  

**Why not**
- Requires consistent input  
- Predictions may be noisy  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
