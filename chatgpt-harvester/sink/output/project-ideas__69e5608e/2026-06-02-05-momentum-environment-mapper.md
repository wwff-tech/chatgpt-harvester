# 5. Momentum Environment Mapper


## Problem Statement

ADHD communities increasingly describe success as:

- environmental,
- social,
- sensory,

rather than organisational. 

Most productivity tools optimise tasks.

Many users actually need activation conditions.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Track:

- lighting,
- location,
- sound,
- devices,
- presence of others.

Identify successful work conditions.

### Approach B

Generate momentum recipes.

Examples:

- headphones on,
- standing desk,
- body-double session,
- ugly-first-draft mode.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Local-first storage
- Wearable integrations
- Behavioural analytics
- Environmental sensing
- Privacy-preserving ML

## Suggested Formats

- Desktop app
- Mobile app
- Raycast extension
- Obsidian plugin

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Distinct from task managers
- Strong ADHD relevance
- Strong self-experimentation appeal

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Noisy behavioural data
- Long-term retention challenge
