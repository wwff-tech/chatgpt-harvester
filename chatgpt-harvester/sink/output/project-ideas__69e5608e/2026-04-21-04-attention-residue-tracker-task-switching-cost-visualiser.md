## 4. “Attention Residue Tracker” (Task Switching Cost Visualiser)


**Problem**  
Task switching leaves cognitive residue, especially in ADHD contexts, but it’s invisible and unmeasured.

**Approaches**
- Track context switches (window/app changes)
- Estimate recovery time via heuristics
- Visualise “lost focus budget”

**Tech hints**
- OS hooks (pyobjc, dbus, etc.)
- Time-series analysis
- Lightweight rules engine

**Formats**
- Local daemon + dashboard
- Daily report generator
- Optional CLI summaries

**Why good fit**
- Directly aligned with your cognitive optimisation angle
- Quantifies a known but poorly measured effect

**Why not**
- Signal extraction is noisy
- Risk of over-quantifying human behaviour

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium

**Content potential**
- Very high

**Community potential**
- High in neurodivergent/productivity spaces

`attention`, `adhd`, `focus`, `cognitive-load`
