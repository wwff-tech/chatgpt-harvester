# 6. Cognitive Lineage Tracker


**Problem**  
People forget how they arrived at decisions or beliefs, leading to inconsistency and poor learning.

**Approaches**
- Track decision inputs and reasoning  
- Link outcomes to prior thinking  
- Enable retrospective analysis  

**Tech hints**
- Journaling + structured prompts  
- Timeline visualisation  
- Local-first storage  

**Formats**
- Desktop app  
- CLI journaling tool  
- Weekly reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD/ASD relevance  
- High introspection value  

**Why not**
- Behavioural overhead  
- Subjective data  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High

`cognition`, `tracking`, `decision-making`, `adhd`
