# 6. Recovery-Aware Productivity System


**Problem**  
Most productivity systems optimise:
- output,
- tasks completed,
- streaks.

But ignore:
- cognitive recovery,
- burnout accumulation,
- recovery debt.

Particularly relevant for ADHD/ASD users.

**Approaches**
- Measure recovery debt alongside productivity  
- Model cognitive load and recovery cycles  
- Recommend pacing rather than maximisation  

**Tech hints**
- Local-first storage  
- Wearable integration  
- Context-switch tracking  
- Task energy estimation  

**Formats**
- Mobile app  
- Desktop companion  
- Weekly cognitive analytics reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong neurodivergent relevance  
- Differentiated from mainstream productivity apps  
- Excellent content potential  

**Why not**
- Behaviourally difficult  
- Recovery metrics are fuzzy  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Extremely high  

**Community potential**  
Very high
