# 4. Governance Maturity Scanner


## Problem Statement

Current enterprise discussions increasingly suggest that governance maturity determines whether agent deployments survive beyond pilot projects. 

Many organisations have:

- agents,
- tools,
- infrastructure,

but lack:

- accountability,
- permission models,
- rollback mechanisms,
- auditability.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Score organisations against governance maturity levels.

Assess:

- identity,
- approvals,
- observability,
- policy controls.

### Approach B

Generate governance roadmaps.

Identify the next highest-leverage improvements.
