# 5. Regulated Presence Companion


## Problem Statement

Recent ADHD discussions repeatedly converge on:

- activation,
- body doubling,
- regulated presence,
- reducing start-up friction. 

Traditional productivity systems optimise:

- organisation,
- planning,
- reminders.

The bottleneck often exists before execution begins.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Create passive presence systems.

Remain visible without demanding interaction.

### Approach B

Continuously detect activation stalls.

Offer:

- structured starts,
- body-doubling sessions,
- momentum recovery interventions.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Local-first analytics
- Webcam presence detection
- Voice interfaces
- Wearables
- Privacy-preserving ML

## Suggested Formats

- Desktop companion
- Mobile app
- Smart display utility
- Mixed-reality assistant

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD relevance
- Narrowly focused value proposition
- Distinct from generic productivity tools

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Difficult behavioural validation
- Requires habit formation
