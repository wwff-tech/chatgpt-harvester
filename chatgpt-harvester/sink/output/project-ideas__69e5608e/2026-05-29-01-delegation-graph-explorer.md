# 1. Delegation Graph Explorer


## Problem Statement

Modern organisations increasingly contain:

- humans,
- AI agents,
- automations,
- workflows,
- MCP servers,
- delegated credentials.

Most organisations cannot answer:

> Who delegated what to whom?

Nor can they easily trace:

- authority,
- ownership,
- responsibility,
- execution history.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Build delegation graphs showing:

- initiator,
- delegate,
- permissions,
- tools,
- outcomes.

### Approach B

Create replayable delegation timelines.

Visualise chains of decisions and actions.
