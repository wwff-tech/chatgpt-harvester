# 5. Context Momentum Tracker


## Problem Statement

Many ADHD productivity systems optimise:

- task completion,
- reminders,
- planning.

They largely ignore:

> activation energy.

Research and community discussions continue to emphasise body doubling, external structure, and interruption recovery as central productivity factors. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Track:

- start latency,
- interruption recovery time,
- context reacquisition time.

### Approach B

Predict momentum collapse and recommend interventions:

- body doubling,
- environmental changes,
- micro-task decomposition.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- local telemetry
- attention state modelling
- activity capture
- local embeddings
- privacy-preserving analytics

## Suggested Formats

- desktop utility
- Raycast extension
- Obsidian plugin
- browser companion

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Differentiated from generic task managers
- Strong neurodivergent relevance
- Useful for engineers and researchers

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Behaviour modelling is noisy
- Requires careful privacy design
