# 7. Leftfield: “Market Instability Early Warning Tool”


**Problem**  
Financial and economic systems exhibit instability before major events, but signals are diffuse.

**Approaches**
- Track leading indicators  
- Detect oscillatory or unstable patterns  
- Provide early warnings  

**Tech hints**
- Time-series modelling  
- Signal processing  
- External data APIs  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- Alerting system  
- Research tool  

**Why good fit**
- High-impact domain  
- Strong analytical appeal  

**Why not**
- Noisy signals  
- Prediction difficulty

Very high

Medium
