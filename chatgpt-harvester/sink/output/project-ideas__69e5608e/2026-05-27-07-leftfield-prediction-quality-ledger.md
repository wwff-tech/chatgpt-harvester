# 7. Leftfield: Prediction Quality Ledger


## Problem Statement

Teams continuously make predictions:

- incident risk,
- staffing needs,
- delivery dates,
- scaling forecasts,
- architectural expectations.

Most organisations never measure forecasting quality systematically.

Poor predictions disappear into history.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Track every prediction with:

- confidence,
- evidence,
- assumptions,
- outcome.

### Approach B

Build organisational forecasting profiles.

Identify:

- overconfidence,
- optimism bias,
- recurring blind spots.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- calibration metrics
- evidence graphs

- forecasting analytics

## Suggested Formats
