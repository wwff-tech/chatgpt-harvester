# 1. Approval Load Balancer


## Problem Statement

Human approval systems increasingly become bottlenecks.

Agent governance discussions repeatedly highlight a failure mode:

- too many approvals → humans ignore them,
- too few approvals → agents become dangerous. 

Most systems treat approval as static.

Risk is dynamic.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Continuously adjust approval requirements based on:

- confidence,
- blast radius,
- historical reliability,
- operational context.

### Approach B

Introduce approval budgets.

Agents earn or lose autonomy according to observed behaviour.

- Risk scoring engines

- Reinforcement learning
