## 2. Escalation Policy Simulator


**Problem**  
Escalation policies are rarely tested under realistic conditions, leading to failures during incidents.

**Approaches**
- Simulate incident scenarios  
- Model escalation timing and outcomes  
- Identify bottlenecks and gaps  

**Tech hints**
- Discrete event simulation  
- Policy-as-code  
- Timeline visualisation  

**Formats**
- CLI simulator  
- Training tool  
- Incident preparedness dashboard  

**Why good fit**
- Strong SRE/process alignment  
- Bridges theory and practice  

**Why not**
- Requires realistic incident modelling  
- Limited by assumptions  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium
