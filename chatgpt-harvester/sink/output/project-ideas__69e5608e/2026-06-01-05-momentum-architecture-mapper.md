# 5. Momentum Architecture Mapper


## Problem Statement

ADHD productivity discussions continue to focus on:

- activation,
- momentum,
- social presence,
- environmental support,

rather than organisation alone. 

Most productivity tools optimise planning.

Many users struggle with task initiation and continuity.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Model conditions associated with successful momentum.

Track:

- location,
- time,
- people present,
- noise,
- device usage.

### Approach B

Generate personalised momentum architectures.

Recommend:

- work patterns,
- body-doubling modes,
- environmental adjustments.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- local-first analytics
- behavioural event streams
- wearable integrations
- time-series modelling

## Suggested Formats

- desktop utility
- mobile app
- Obsidian plugin
- Raycast extension

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD relevance
- Behaviour-focused
- Privacy-friendly implementations possible

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Long feedback loops
- Behavioural variability
