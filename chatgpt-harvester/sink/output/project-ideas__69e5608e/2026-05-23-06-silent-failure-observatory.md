# 6. Silent Failure Observatory


## Problem Statement

Traditional monitoring detects:

- crashes,
- errors,
- outages.

Modern AI systems increasingly fail silently:

- degraded reasoning,
- hallucinations,
- tool misuse,
- escalating costs,
- behavioural drift.

This is becoming a major observability theme. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Continuously score:

- response quality,
- consistency,
- objective completion.

### Approach B

Detect behavioural drift over time.

Alert before visible failures emerge.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- evaluation pipelines
- statistical baselines
- embeddings
- drift detection
- replay testing

## Suggested Formats

- AgentOps platform
- observability add-on
- governance dashboard
- CI validation system

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong demand trajectory
- AI-native observability gap
- Useful across vendors

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Evaluation remains difficult
- Ground truth often unavailable
