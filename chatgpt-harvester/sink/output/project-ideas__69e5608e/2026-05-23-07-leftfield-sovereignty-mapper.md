# 7. Leftfield: Sovereignty Mapper


## Problem Statement

Increasing discussions around:

- AI governance,
- data residency,
- supply-chain risk,
- geopolitical technology dependencies,

are pushing organisations toward infrastructure sovereignty concerns. 

Most organisations cannot answer:

> Which countries, providers, regulations, and dependencies influence this workload?

## Approaches

### Approach A

Map:

- vendors,
- cloud services,
- models,
- regions,
- regulatory exposure.

### Approach B

Simulate geopolitical failure scenarios.

Estimate resilience and exposure.
