## 4. “Interrupt Cost Calculator” (Real-Time Cognitive Tax Meter)


**Problem**  
Interruptions impose hidden cognitive costs, especially in ADHD contexts, but are not quantified in real time.

**Approaches**
- Detect interruptions (notifications, app switches)
- Estimate recovery cost using heuristics
- Provide real-time “cost feedback”

**Tech hints**
- OS-level hooks (cross-platform abstraction)
- Time-series tracking
- Lightweight rules engine

**Formats**
- Local daemon
- Overlay widget
- Daily/weekly reports

**Why good fit**
- Strong personal relevance
- Extends previous “latency” and “residue” ideas into real-time feedback

**Why not**
- Risk of becoming intrusive
- Estimation accuracy is limited

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium

**Content potential**
- Very high

**Community potential**
- High

`adhd`, `interruptions`, `focus`, `cognitive-cost`
