# 5. Executive Function Delegation Assistant


## Problem Statement

A recurring ADHD theme in 2026:

> Planning is not the problem. Starting is.

Body doubling and regulated presence continue to gain attention because they help with executive-function delegation rather than task management. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Create an AI body-double.

Focus on:

- activation,
- transitions,
- momentum maintenance.

### Approach B

Track delegation patterns:

- what the user can initiate,
- what needs support,
- when intervention helps.

Generate personalised activation strategies.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Local-first storage
- Voice interaction
- Wearable integrations
- Behavioural analytics
- Context-aware reminders

## Suggested Formats

- Desktop companion
- Mobile app
- Raycast extension
- Smart speaker integration

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD/ASD relevance
- Different from traditional task managers
- Consumer market opportunity

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Behavioural outcomes difficult to measure
- Requires long-term engagement
