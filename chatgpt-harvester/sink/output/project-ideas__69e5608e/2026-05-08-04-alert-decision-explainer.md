# 4. Alert Decision Explainer


**Problem**  
Engineers receive alerts without understanding:
- why this alert fired,
- why others were suppressed,
- what changed.

**Approaches**
- Attach decision explanations to alerts  
- Show triggering conditions and alternatives  
- Provide “why now?” context  

**Tech hints**
- Rule tracing  
- Threshold change tracking  
- Integration with Prometheus and Grafana  

**Formats**
- Alerting plugin  
- Slack/Teams bot  
- Dashboard overlay  

**Why good fit**
- Reduces alert fatigue  
- Improves trust in systems  

**Why not**
- Complex explanation generation  
- May add noise if poorly designed  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium → High  

**Content potential**  
High  

**Community potential**  
High
