## 1. “Uncertainty Gate” (Pause Before Risky Actions)


**Problem**  
Systems and agents execute actions even when confidence is low or signals are ambiguous, leading to avoidable failures.

**Approaches**
- Introduce uncertainty thresholds before execution  
- Require additional validation or consensus when confidence is low  
- Escalate to human-in-the-loop selectively  

**Tech hints**
- Confidence scoring aggregation  
- Policy engine for gating decisions  
- Integration with CI/CD or agent pipelines  

**Formats**
- Middleware layer  
- Agent framework plugin  
- CLI validation tool  

**Why good fit**
- Strong alignment with AI safety and SRE reliability  
- Simple concept, high leverage  

**Why not**
- Adds latency  
- Risk of over-blocking legitimate actions  

**Revenue potential**
- High  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
