# 6. “Goal-Coherence Planner (Human)”


**Problem**  
People optimise daily tasks without aligning with long-term goals:
- busy but ineffective,  
- fragmented effort,  
- lack of strategic progress.

**Approaches**
- Link tasks to goals explicitly  
- Score daily actions against long-term objectives  
- Provide course correction  

**Tech hints**
- Task graph modelling  
- Periodic review prompts  
- Local-first storage  

**Formats**
- Mobile/desktop app  
- CLI planner  
- Weekly reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong self-improvement angle  
- High behavioural impact  

**Why not**
- Requires discipline  
- Subjective scoring  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High
