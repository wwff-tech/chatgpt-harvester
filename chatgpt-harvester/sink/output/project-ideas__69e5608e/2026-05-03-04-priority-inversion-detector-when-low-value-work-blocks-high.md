## 4. “Priority Inversion Detector” (When Low-Value Work Blocks High-Value Work)


**Problem**  
Low-priority tasks or processes block higher-priority ones, both in systems and human workflows.

**Approaches**
- Detect blocking relationships  
- Identify misaligned priorities  
- Suggest reordering or isolation  

**Tech hints**
- Dependency graph analysis  
- Scheduling models  
- Resource contention tracking  

**Formats**
- CLI analyser  
- Dashboard  
- Personal productivity tool  

**Why good fit**
- Cross-domain applicability  
- Strong systems thinking alignment  

**Why not**
- Requires accurate dependency modelling  
- May produce noisy results  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium
