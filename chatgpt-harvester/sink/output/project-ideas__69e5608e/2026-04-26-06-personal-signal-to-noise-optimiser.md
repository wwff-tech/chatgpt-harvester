## 6. “Personal Signal-to-Noise Optimiser”


**Problem**  
Information intake (news, feeds, notifications) is high-noise, reducing learning efficiency and focus.

**Approaches**
- Track content consumption
- Score signal vs noise (based on engagement + outcomes)
- Filter or prioritise inputs

**Tech hints**
- Browser extension + local storage
- Simple scoring heuristics
- Optional ML ranking

**Formats**
- Browser plugin
- CLI summariser
- Daily digest generator

**Why good fit**
- Directly relevant to self-improvement
- Aligns with your optimisation mindset

**Why not**
- Subjective scoring
- Risk of over-filtering

**Revenue potential**
- Medium

**Content potential**
- High

**Community potential**
- Medium → High

`information`, `productivity`, `filtering`, `attention`
