# 3. Machine-Native Postmortems


## Problem Statement

Current postmortems are written for humans.

Future incident responders increasingly include:

- AI agents,
- copilots,
- automated investigators.

Observability systems are shifting toward machine consumers of operational knowledge. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Generate structured incident records:

- causal graph,
- remediation path,
- decision points,
- confidence scores.

### Approach B

Create incident datasets that can be queried directly by agents.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Knowledge graphs
- Event sourcing
- OpenTelemetry
- Graph databases
- Vector retrieval

## Suggested Formats

- Incident platform
- SRE tooling
- Postmortem assistant
- Open-source schema standard

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong SRE relevance
- AI-native future
- Reusable operational memory

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Requires disciplined incident practices
- Causality remains hard
