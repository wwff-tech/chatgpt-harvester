# 7. Leftfield: “Civilisation Latency Map”


## Problem
Modern societies increasingly optimise:
- efficiency,
- just-in-time systems,
- responsiveness.

But reduce:
- slack,
- redundancy,
- recovery margins.

A hidden issue:
> civilisation-wide latency sensitivity.

## Approaches
- Model dependency chains across infrastructure sectors  
- Simulate cascading latency amplification  
- Visualise fragility hotspots  

## Suggested tech hints
- Graph simulations  
- Public infrastructure datasets  
- System dynamics modelling  
- Supply chain telemetry  

## Suggested formats
- Research platform  
- Interactive map  
- Educational simulation  

## Why it is a good fit
- Strong systems-thinking appeal  
- Highly differentiated intellectually  

## Why it is not a good fit
- Hard monetisation  
- Massive modelling scope
