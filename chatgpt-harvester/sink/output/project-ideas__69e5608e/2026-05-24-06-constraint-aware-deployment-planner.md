# 6. Constraint-Aware Deployment Planner


## Problem Statement

Deployment systems optimise:

- speed,
- frequency,
- automation.

Failures often occur because hidden constraints are ignored:

- support coverage,
- dependency readiness,
- business events,
- staffing,
- external providers.

Technical readiness is not operational readiness.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Build deployment risk models using organisational and technical constraints.

### Approach B

Continuously assess readiness based on:

- staffing,
- incident load,
- service health,
- dependency state.
