# 6. Reliability Narrative Generator


## Problem Statement

Reliability is increasingly measured by:

- trust,
- resilience,
- user experience,
- business impact,

rather than uptime alone. 

Most observability systems explain *what happened*.

Few explain:

> Why does this matter?

## Approaches

### Approach A

Convert telemetry into stakeholder narratives:

- engineering
- product
- leadership
- customer success

### Approach B

Build causally-linked explanations connecting:

- incidents,
- customer outcomes,
- revenue,
- operational decisions.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Causal graphs
- LLM summarisation
- Business telemetry correlation
- Event sourcing
- Knowledge graphs

## Suggested Formats

- Executive dashboard
- Incident review platform
- Board reporting tool

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Executive visibility
- Growing business observability trend
- Strong storytelling value

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Attribution complexity
- Requires cross-functional data
