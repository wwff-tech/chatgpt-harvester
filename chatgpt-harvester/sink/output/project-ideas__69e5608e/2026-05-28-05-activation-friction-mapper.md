# 5. Activation Friction Mapper


## Problem Statement

ADHD productivity discussions increasingly emphasise:

- transition cost,
- activation friction,
- environmental resistance,
- regulated presence,
- body doubling. 

Traditional productivity tooling measures:
- tasks,
- deadlines,
- completion rates.

The real failure often occurs before initiation.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Map activation friction sources:

- workspace state,
- interruptions,
- sensory load,
- ambiguity,
- digital clutter.

### Approach B

Continuously learn:
- successful activation conditions,
- low-friction sequences,
- momentum-preserving workflows.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- local-first analytics
- behavioural telemetry
- environmental sensing
- time-series analysis
- privacy-preserving ML

## Suggested Formats

- desktop utility
- mobile app
- Raycast plugin
- browser extension

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD/ASD relevance
- Distinct from generic productivity tooling
- Strong self-experimentation potential

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Behavioural variability
