## 7. Leftfield: “Narrative Bias Detector”


**Problem**  
Humans (and AI) construct narratives that oversimplify or misrepresent complex systems.

**Approaches**
- Analyse explanations vs underlying data  
- Detect oversimplification or bias patterns  
- Provide alternative interpretations  

**Tech hints**
- NLP techniques for narrative analysis  
- Comparison against raw data  
- Heuristic bias detection  

**Formats**
- Writing assistant tool  
- CLI analyser  
- Browser extension  

**Why good fit**
- Unique and intellectually rich  
- Applies to both humans and AI outputs  

**Why not**
- Hard to define “correct narrative”  
- Subjective domain  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- Medium

`bias`, `narrative`, `analysis`, `thinking`
