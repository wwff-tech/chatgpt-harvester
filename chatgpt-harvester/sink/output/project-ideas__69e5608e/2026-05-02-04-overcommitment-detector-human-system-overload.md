## 4. “Overcommitment Detector” (Human System Overload)


**Problem**  
People commit to more tasks than they can realistically handle, especially under ADHD-related impulsivity or optimism bias.

**Approaches**
- Compare planned workload vs historical throughput  
- Detect overcommitment patterns  
- Suggest constraint-based planning  

**Tech hints**
- Time-series analysis of completed tasks  
- Throughput modelling  
- Simple predictive heuristics  

**Formats**
- Personal productivity app  
- CLI + reports  
- Integration with task managers  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD relevance  
- Practical and actionable  

**Why not**
- Requires historical data  
- Behaviour change needed  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
