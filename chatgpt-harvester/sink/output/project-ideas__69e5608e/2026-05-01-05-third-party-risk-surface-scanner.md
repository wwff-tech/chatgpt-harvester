## 5. Third-Party Risk Surface Scanner


**Problem**  
Modern systems depend heavily on third-party APIs/services, but their risk surface is poorly understood.

**Approaches**
- Map external dependencies  
- Assess risk (availability, data exposure, jurisdiction)  
- Monitor changes over time  

**Tech hints**
- Dependency scanning  
- API usage analysis  
- External metadata (uptime, location, compliance)  

**Formats**
- CLI audit tool  
- Dashboard  
- CI/CD integration  

**Why good fit**
- Strong security/privacy alignment  
- Increasing reliance on SaaS  

**Why not**
- Limited visibility into third parties  
- Data freshness challenges  

**Revenue potential**
- High  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium → High
