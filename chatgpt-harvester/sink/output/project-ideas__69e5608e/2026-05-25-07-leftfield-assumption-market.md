# 7. Leftfield: Assumption Market


## Problem Statement

Every organisation operates on assumptions:

- customer behaviour,
- traffic forecasts,
- security models,
- staffing expectations,
- architectural decisions.

Most assumptions remain invisible until they fail.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Treat assumptions as trackable assets.

Assign:

- owner,
- confidence,
- evidence,
- expiry date.

### Approach B

Create organisational markets where assumptions gain or lose confidence based on operational evidence.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Knowledge graphs

- Evidence linking
