# 5. “Privacy Risk Confidence Meter”


**Problem**  
Systems process sensitive data without expressing uncertainty about privacy risk.

**Approaches**
- Score risk of data exposure per operation  
- Provide confidence intervals for risk  
- Trigger safeguards when uncertainty is high  

**Tech hints**
- PII detection models  
- Risk scoring frameworks  
- Integration with data pipelines and APIs  

**Formats**
- API middleware  
- Security dashboard  
- CLI audit tool  

**Why good fit**
- Strong compliance relevance  
- Differentiated vs binary “allow/deny” systems  

**Why not**
- Risk modelling is subjective  
- Potential regulatory scrutiny  

**Revenue potential**  
High  

**Content potential**  
High  

**Community potential**  
Medium
