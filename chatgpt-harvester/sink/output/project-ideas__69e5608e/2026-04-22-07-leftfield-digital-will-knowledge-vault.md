## 7. Leftfield: “Digital Will & Knowledge Vault”


**Problem**  
People accumulate digital assets (code, notes, systems), but there’s no structured way to transfer knowledge or access after death/incapacity.

**Approaches**
- Encrypted vault with staged access controls
- Dead-man switch mechanism
- Structured “knowledge export” system

**Tech hints**
- End-to-end encryption (libsodium)
- Secure storage (local-first + optional cloud backup)
- Key escrow mechanisms

**Formats**
- CLI + web UI
- Secure vault app
- Documentation generator

**Why good fit**
- Ethically interesting, non-trivial problem
- Combines security + human concerns

**Why not**
- Sensitive domain (legal implications)
- Trust is critical

**Revenue potential**
- Medium

**Content potential**
- High

**Community potential**
- Medium

`security`, `encryption`, `legacy`, `knowledge`
