## 5. Data Retention Risk Scanner


**Problem**  
Systems accumulate data indefinitely, increasing privacy risk and regulatory exposure.

**Approaches**
- Scan databases, logs, storage for retention patterns
- Identify data exceeding defined policies
- Suggest deletion/anonymisation strategies

**Tech hints**
- Python + database connectors
- Pattern matching for PII
- Policy engine (retention rules)

**Formats**
- CLI audit tool
- Scheduled job
- Dashboard

**Why good fit**
- Strong privacy/security alignment
- Increasing regulatory pressure (GDPR, etc.)

**Why not**
- Requires access to sensitive data (security risk)
- Hard to classify data accurately

**Revenue potential**
- High

**Content potential**
- High

**Community potential**
- Medium → High
