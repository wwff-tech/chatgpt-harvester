## 6. Task Interference Detector


**Problem**  
Tasks interfere with each other (shared resources, context overlap), reducing efficiency and increasing errors.

**Approaches**
- Detect overlapping resource usage  
- Identify conflicting task characteristics  
- Suggest sequencing or isolation  

**Tech hints**
- Resource tracking  
- Heuristic interference models  
- Scheduling algorithms  

**Formats**
- Personal productivity tool  
- CLI analyser  
- Integration with task managers  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD relevance  
- Extends beyond simple task tracking  

**Why not**
- Hard to model interference accurately  
- Behavioural adoption needed  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
