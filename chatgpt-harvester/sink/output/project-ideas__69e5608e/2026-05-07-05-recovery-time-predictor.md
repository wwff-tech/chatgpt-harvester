# 5. “Recovery Time Predictor”


**Problem**  
Teams cannot reliably estimate how long recovery will take during incidents.

**Approaches**
- Analyse historical incidents  
- Model recovery time distributions  
- Provide real-time estimates during incidents  

**Tech hints**
- Time-series modelling  
- Incident classification  
- Statistical prediction (Bayesian models)  

**Formats**
- Dashboard widget  
- CLI tool  
- Incident platform integration  

**Why good fit**
- Practical and measurable  
- Improves decision-making under pressure  

**Why not**
- Data quality dependency  
- High variance in incidents  

**Revenue potential**
Medium  

**Content potential**
High  

**Community potential**
Medium
