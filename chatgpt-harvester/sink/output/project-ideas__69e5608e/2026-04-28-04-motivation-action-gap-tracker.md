## 4. Motivation-Action Gap Tracker


**Problem**  
People often know what to do but fail to act; the gap between intent and execution is poorly understood.

**Approaches**
- Track declared intentions vs actual actions  
- Identify patterns of drop-off  
- Suggest micro-adjustments  

**Tech hints**
- Local tracking + manual input  
- Time-series comparison  
- Heuristic scoring  

**Formats**
- Mobile-friendly app  
- CLI + reports  
- Integration with task tools  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD/self-improvement relevance  
- Focuses on behavioural reality  

**Why not**
- Requires honest input  
- Behaviour change is hard  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
