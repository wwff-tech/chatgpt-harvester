# 6. Reliability Trust Score


## Problem Statement

Reliability is increasingly defined by:

- user trust,
- predictability,
- consistency,
- business impact,

rather than uptime alone. 

Most organisations still optimise technical metrics disconnected from user experience.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Build trust models combining:

- incidents,
- latency,
- support volume,
- customer sentiment,
- behavioural metrics.

### Approach B

Estimate trust degradation before users complain.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Causal inference
- Time-series forecasting
- Product analytics
- Observability pipelines
- Customer telemetry

## Suggested Formats

- Executive dashboard
- Reliability platform
- Product analytics extension

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Business-aligned

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit
