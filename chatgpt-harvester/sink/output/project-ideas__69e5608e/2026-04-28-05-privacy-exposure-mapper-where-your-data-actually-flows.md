## 5. “Privacy Exposure Mapper” (Where Your Data Actually Flows)


**Problem**  
Data flows across systems are poorly understood, leading to unintended exposure and compliance risk.

**Approaches**
- Map data lineage across services  
- Identify external exposures  
- Highlight high-risk paths  

**Tech hints**
- Log analysis + tracing  
- Graph modelling  
- PII detection heuristics  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- CLI audit tool  
- Compliance report generator  

**Why good fit**
- Strong security/privacy alignment  
- Increasing regulatory importance  

**Why not**
- Requires deep system access  
- Complex to model accurately  

**Revenue potential**
- High  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium → High
