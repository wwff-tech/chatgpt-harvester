## 6. “Execution Confidence Scorer”


**Problem**  
Systems execute actions but provide no measure of confidence in correctness.

**Approaches**
- Combine multiple signals (validation checks, model certainty, consistency)  
- Produce confidence score per action  
- Threshold-based escalation  

**Tech hints**
- Weighted scoring models  
- Aggregation pipelines  
- Optional ML calibration  

**Formats**
- Middleware layer  
- CLI tool  
- Dashboard  

**Why good fit**
- Aligns with probabilistic system reality  
- Useful across domains  

**Why not**
- Confidence ≠ correctness  
- Calibration is difficult

- Medium

- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium
