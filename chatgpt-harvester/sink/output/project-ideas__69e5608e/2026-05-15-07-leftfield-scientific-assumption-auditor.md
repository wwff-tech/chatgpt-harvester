# 7. Leftfield: “Scientific Assumption Auditor”


**Problem**  
Scientific models rely on assumptions that may become outdated or invalid.

**Approaches**
- Extract assumptions from papers/models  
- Track their validity over time  
- Highlight fragile or outdated assumptions  

**Tech hints**
- NLP for claim extraction  
- Knowledge graphs  
- Temporal validation  

**Formats**
- Research tool  
- Dashboard  
- API  

**Why good fit**
- High intellectual value  
- Useful for research and policy  

**Why not**
- Complex data extraction  
- Ambiguity in interpretation  

**Revenue potential**  
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium
