# 7. Leftfield: “Counterfactual Simulator” (What If We Chose Differently?)


**Problem**  
Systems and organisations rarely explore:
- what would have happened under different decisions,
- missed opportunities,
- alternative strategies.

**Approaches**
- Replay decision timelines with alternative branches  
- Simulate outcomes under different policies  
- Compare real vs counterfactual results  

**Tech hints**
- Simulation frameworks  
- Decision trees + branching timelines  
- Probabilistic modelling  

**Formats**
- Simulation dashboard  
- CLI tool  
- Research platform  

**Why good fit**
- Extremely powerful for learning  
- Cross-domain applicability  

**Why not**
- Complex modelling  
- Requires assumptions  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium
