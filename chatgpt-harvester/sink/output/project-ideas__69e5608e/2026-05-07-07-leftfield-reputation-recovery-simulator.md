# 7. Leftfield: “Reputation Recovery Simulator”


**Problem**  
Organisations and individuals struggle to recover from reputational damage, with little structured analysis of recovery strategies.

**Approaches**
- Model reputation trajectories after incidents  
- Simulate different response strategies  
- Analyse long-term outcomes  

**Tech hints**
- Sentiment analysis  
- Time-series modelling  
- Scenario simulation  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- Research tool  
- Strategy simulator  

**Why good fit**
- Unique and interdisciplinary  
- Applies to media, tech, and public figures  

**Why not**
- Subjective modelling  
- Ethical considerations  

**Revenue potential**
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
Very high  

**Community potential**
Medium
