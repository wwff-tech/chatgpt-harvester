# 6. “Personal Decision Journal (Automatic)”


**Problem**  
People make many small decisions daily without reflection, limiting learning and self-improvement.

**Approaches**
- Automatically capture decisions (task switches, prioritisation)  
- Periodically review outcomes  
- Suggest improvements  

**Tech hints**
- Activity tracking  
- Lightweight inference of decisions  
- Local-first storage for privacy  

**Formats**
- Desktop app  
- CLI journaling tool  
- Weekly report generator  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD/self-improvement relevance  
- High introspection value  

**Why not**
- Inference accuracy challenges  
- Behavioural adoption needed  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High
