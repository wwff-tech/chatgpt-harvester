# 1. Runbook Compiler


## Problem Statement

Most operational runbooks are:

- written for humans,
- poorly maintained,
- difficult for agents to use,
- disconnected from reality.

At the same time, observability systems increasingly expect AI agents to consume operational knowledge directly. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Convert traditional runbooks into machine-readable workflows.

Generate:

- decision trees,
- remediation actions,
- escalation paths,
- rollback procedures.

### Approach B

Continuously validate runbooks against actual incidents.

Detect:

- stale instructions,
- missing dependencies,
- incorrect assumptions.
