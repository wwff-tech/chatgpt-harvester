## 6. Cognitive Context Switch Predictor


**Problem**  
Context switches are often reactive; predicting them could reduce disruption.

**Approaches**
- Model patterns of switching (time, triggers)  
- Predict likely switches  
- Pre-emptively adjust workload  

**Tech hints**
- Time-series modelling  
- Simple ML models (logistic regression, decision trees)  
- Local tracking  

**Formats**
- Desktop assistant  
- CLI + alerts  
- Calendar integration  

**Why good fit**
- Extends ADHD tooling into predictive space  
- Novel angle on focus optimisation  

**Why not**
- Prediction accuracy may be limited  
- Risk of over-intervention  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
