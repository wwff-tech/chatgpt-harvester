# 7. Leftfield: “Epistemic Health Monitor”


**Problem**  
Individuals and organisations degrade in decision quality due to poor epistemic practices (bias, poor evidence handling, echo chambers).

**Approaches**
- Track sources of information and decisions  
- Score epistemic quality (diversity, evidence strength)  
- Highlight weak reasoning patterns  

**Tech hints**
- Knowledge graphs  
- Source classification  
- Heuristic scoring models  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- Browser extension  
- Research tool  

**Why good fit**
- Highly differentiated  
- Increasing societal relevance  

**Why not**
- Subjective metrics  
- Risk of misuse or bias  

**Revenue potential**  
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium  

**Tags**  
`epistemics`, `reasoning`, `analysis`, `decision-quality`
