# 7. Leftfield: “Media Narrative Lineage Tracker”


**Problem**  
News narratives evolve over time, but the lineage of claims and framing is difficult to trace.

**Approaches**
- Track how stories change across sources  
- Link claims to original reporting  
- Visualise narrative evolution  

**Tech hints**
- NLP for claim extraction  
- Temporal knowledge graphs  
- Source clustering  

**Formats**
- Research dashboard  
- Media analysis tool  
- API  

**Why good fit**
- Highly differentiated  
- Societal relevance  

**Why not**
- Data ingestion challenges  
- Political sensitivity  

**Revenue potential**  
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium

`media`, `analysis`, `narratives`, `tracking`
