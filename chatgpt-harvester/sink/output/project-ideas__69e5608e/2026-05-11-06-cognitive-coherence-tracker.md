# 6. Cognitive Coherence Tracker


**Problem**  
Humans pursue goals that conflict over time due to context switching, interruptions, and shifting priorities.

**Approaches**
- Track goals and actions over time  
- Detect inconsistencies  
- Suggest alignment strategies  

**Tech hints**
- Goal graph modelling  
- Activity tracking  
- Periodic review summaries  

**Formats**
- Desktop app  
- CLI journaling tool  
- Weekly reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD/ASD relevance  
- High personal utility  

**Why not**
- Requires behavioural discipline  
- Subjective interpretation  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High
