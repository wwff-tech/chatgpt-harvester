# 7. Leftfield: Synthetic Consensus Detector


## Problem Statement

As AI-generated content increases, organisations increasingly struggle to determine whether:
- agreement is organic,
- telemetry is authentic,
- reviews are human,
- operational signals are synthetic amplification.

The problem extends beyond social media into:
- observability,
- support systems,
- incident management,
- governance processes.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Detect consensus anomalies:
- linguistic similarity,
- timing correlation,
- behavioural coordination.

### Approach B

Construct confidence layers for:
- alerts,
- operational reports,
- community feedback,
- internal recommendations.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- graph analytics

- anomaly detection

## Suggested Formats
