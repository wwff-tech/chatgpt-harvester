# 5. Momentum Transfer System


## Problem Statement

ADHD productivity discussions increasingly centre on:

- activation,
- momentum,
- accountability,
- body doubling.

The problem is often not planning.

It is preserving motion once work begins. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Track momentum transitions:

- what causes starts,
- what causes stalls,
- what restores flow.

### Approach B

Create structured momentum hand-offs between:

- tasks,
- environments,
- sessions,
- collaborators.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Local-first analytics
- Wearable integrations
- Behavioural event tracking
- Context classification
- Privacy-preserving ML

## Suggested Formats

- Desktop app
- Mobile app
- Obsidian plugin
- Raycast extension

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Distinct from traditional task management
- Strong ADHD relevance
- Strong personal experimentation appeal

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Signal quality is noisy
- Behavioural adherence challenges
