# 5. Cognitive Resume Generator


## Problem Statement

Knowledge workers lose context after interruptions.

Particularly painful for:

- ADHD,
- ASD,
- consulting,
- engineering,
- research work.

Current tools remember files.

They do not remember thought processes.

Body-doubling, external structure, and cognitive scaffolding remain recurring ADHD productivity themes. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Periodic cognitive checkpoints:

- current goal,
- next step,
- blockers,
- assumptions.

### Approach B

Activity reconstruction:

- browser,
- IDE,
- documents,
- tickets,

then generate a resumable briefing.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- local embeddings
- SQLite/DuckDB
- OS activity capture
- semantic session summaries
- privacy-preserving local models

## Suggested Formats

- desktop app
- browser extension
- Raycast plugin
- menu-bar utility

## Why It Is a Good Fit

- Massive ADHD relevance
- Personal productivity market
- Strong differentiation

## Why It Is Not a Good Fit

- Privacy concerns
- Activity capture complexity
