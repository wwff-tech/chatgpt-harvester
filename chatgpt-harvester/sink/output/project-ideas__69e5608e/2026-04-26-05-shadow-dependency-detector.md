## 5. “Shadow Dependency Detector”


**Problem**  
Systems often rely on implicit dependencies (undocumented APIs, shared resources), creating hidden fragility.

**Approaches**
- Analyse runtime traffic and logs
- Infer undocumented dependencies
- Flag “shadow” relationships

**Tech hints**
- Network tracing
- Log analysis
- Graph modelling

**Formats**
- CLI tool
- Visual graph explorer
- CI audit step

**Why good fit**
- Strong systems thinking alignment
- Helps uncover hidden risks

**Why not**
- Inference accuracy may vary
- Requires good observability

**Revenue potential**
- Medium → High

**Content potential**
- High

**Community potential**
- Medium
