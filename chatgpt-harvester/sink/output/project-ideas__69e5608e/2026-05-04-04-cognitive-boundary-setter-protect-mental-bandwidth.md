## 4. “Cognitive Boundary Setter” (Protect Mental Bandwidth)


**Problem**  
People (especially ADHD/ASD profiles) struggle to enforce boundaries on time, tasks, and attention.

**Approaches**
- Define explicit limits (tasks per day, context switches)  
- Enforce via soft/hard constraints  
- Provide feedback on boundary violations  

**Tech hints**
- Task tracking + limit enforcement  
- Timeboxing integration  
- Behavioural nudges  

**Formats**
- Desktop/mobile app  
- CLI + reports  
- Calendar integration  

**Why good fit**
- Strong neurodivergent relevance  
- Practical and behaviourally grounded  

**Why not**
- Requires discipline  
- Risk of user resistance  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
