## 7. Leftfield: “Personal Reality Drift Tracker”


**Problem**  
People’s perception of their own behaviour (time spent, habits, focus) diverges from reality.

**Approaches**
- Compare self-reported vs actual behaviour
- Highlight discrepancies
- Provide corrective feedback

**Tech hints**
- Combine manual input + passive tracking
- Statistical comparison
- Visualisation tools

**Formats**
- Local-first app
- CLI + reports
- Mobile-friendly UI

**Why good fit**
- Deeply aligned with introspective optimisation
- Novel framing

**Why not**
- Requires honest input
- Potential psychological sensitivity

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium

**Content potential**
- Very high

**Community potential**
- Medium

`self-awareness`, `behaviour`, `tracking`, `analysis`
