# 7. Leftfield: Policy Budget Engine


## Problem Statement

Research is increasingly exploring agent systems where permissions are constrained by budgets rather than static rules. 

Future agent ecosystems may require:

- communication budgets,
- permission budgets,
- risk budgets,
- resource budgets.

Static policy models do not scale well.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Allocate dynamic budgets.

Agents consume authority based on:

- confidence,
- risk,
- context.

### Approach B

Implement delegation markets.

Allow authority to be transferred under defined constraints.
