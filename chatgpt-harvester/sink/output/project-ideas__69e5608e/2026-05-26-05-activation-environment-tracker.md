# 5. Activation Environment Tracker


## Problem Statement

A recurring ADHD insight:

> Starting is often harder than doing.

Recent discussions continue to emphasise:

- activation energy,
- body doubling,
- environmental triggers,
- transition management,

rather than traditional productivity systems. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Track environmental variables:

- location,
- time,
- noise,
- interruptions,
- people present,
- digital context.

Identify activation patterns.

### Approach B

Generate personalised startup recipes:

- workspace setup,
- music,
- timing,
- body-doubling recommendations.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Local-first analytics
- Time-series modelling
- Environmental sensing
- Privacy-preserving storage
- Wearable integrations

## Suggested Formats

- Desktop utility
- Mobile application
- Raycast plugin
- Obsidian integration

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD relevance
- Behaviour-focused rather than task-focused
- Privacy-first differentiation possible

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Behavioural signals are noisy
- Longitudinal data required
