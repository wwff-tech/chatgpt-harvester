# 5. Energy-Aware Productivity System


## Problem Statement

Many productivity tools optimise:

- tasks,
- deadlines,
- priorities.

Neurodivergent communities increasingly discuss:

- energy management,
- recovery cycles,
- activation cost,
- sensory load.

The limiting factor is often energy rather than time.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Model cognitive energy as a first-class resource.

Track:

- activation cost,
- recovery requirement,
- interruption sensitivity.

### Approach B

Recommend work based on predicted energy availability rather than calendar slots.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Local-first storage
- Wearable integrations
- Activity tracking
- Statistical forecasting
- Privacy-preserving analytics

## Suggested Formats

- Mobile application
- Desktop companion
- Obsidian plugin
- Personal analytics dashboard

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD/ASD relevance
- Differentiated from traditional productivity apps
- Behaviourally useful

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Energy modelling remains imperfect
- Requires sustained engagement
