## 6. Interruption Cost Calculator


**Problem**  
Interruptions are treated as binary events, but their *true cost* (recovery time, error risk) is not measured.

**Approaches**
- Track interruptions and recovery time  
- Model cost per interruption  
- Provide optimisation suggestions  

**Tech hints**
- Event tracking (focus changes)  
- Time-to-resume metrics  
- Statistical modelling  

**Formats**
- Desktop tool  
- CLI analyser  
- Weekly report generator  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD relevance  
- Makes hidden cost visible  

**Why not**
- Measurement noise  
- Behaviour change required  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
