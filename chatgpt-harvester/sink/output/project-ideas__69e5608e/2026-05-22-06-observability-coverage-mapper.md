# 6. Observability Coverage Mapper


## Problem Statement

Observability often grows organically.

Teams know what emits telemetry.

They rarely know:

- what does not.

As AI-generated code expands codebases, blind spots become harder to identify. citeturn0news17turn0news21

## Approaches

### Approach A

Map runtime traces to source ownership.

Highlight:

- dark services,
- dark endpoints,
- dark workflows.

### Approach B

Compare execution graphs with telemetry graphs.

Flag uninstrumented paths.
