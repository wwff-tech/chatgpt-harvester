## 7. Leftfield: “Ideological Drift Mapper”


**Problem**  
Groups, communities, or individuals shift beliefs over time without clear visibility into how or why.

**Approaches**
- Track statements/content over time  
- Analyse shifts in position or tone  
- Visualise ideological trajectories  

**Tech hints**
- NLP + embedding analysis  
- Time-series clustering  
- Topic modelling  

**Formats**
- Research tool  
- Dashboard  
- CLI analyser  

**Why good fit**
- Unique, intellectually rich  
- Applicable to media, politics, and communities  

**Why not**
- Sensitive domain  
- Risk of misuse or misinterpretation  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- Medium
