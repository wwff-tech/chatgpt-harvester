# 6. Personal Responsibility Tracker


**Problem**  
Individuals lose track of commitments and responsibilities:
- unclear ownership of tasks,  
- overcommitment,  
- dropped responsibilities.

Particularly relevant for ADHD/ASD profiles.

**Approaches**
- Track commitments with explicit ownership  
- Monitor completion and follow-through  
- Provide accountability feedback  

**Tech hints**
- Task tracking + reminders  
- Behavioural analytics  
- Local-first storage  

**Formats**
- Mobile/desktop app  
- CLI tool  
- Weekly reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong personal utility  
- High behavioural impact  

**Why not**
- Requires consistent use  
- Potential for guilt/pressure  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High
