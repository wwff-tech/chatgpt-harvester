# 6. Cognitive Assumption Tracker


**Problem**  
People operate on unexamined assumptions:
- about time, effort, priorities,  
- about other people’s expectations.

This leads to poor decisions and stress, especially for ADHD/ASD profiles.

**Approaches**
- Prompt users to externalise assumptions  
- Track outcomes vs expectations  
- Provide feedback on inaccurate assumptions  

**Tech hints**
- Lightweight journaling  
- Pattern detection  
- Local-first storage  

**Formats**
- Mobile/desktop app  
- CLI journaling tool  
- Weekly insight reports  

**Why good fit**
- High introspection value  
- Strong behavioural impact  

**Why not**
- Requires consistent use  
- Subjective interpretation  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High
