# 6. “Cognitive Stability Assistant”


**Problem**  
Humans overcorrect behaviour:
- productivity swings,  
- burnout cycles,  
- inconsistent routines.

Particularly relevant for ADHD/ASD profiles.

**Approaches**
- Track behavioural patterns over time  
- Detect oscillations (overwork → crash)  
- Suggest stabilising routines  

**Tech hints**
- Time-series behaviour tracking  
- Pattern detection  
- Local-first data storage  

**Formats**
- Mobile/desktop app  
- CLI tracker  
- Weekly reports  

**Why good fit**
- Strong personal relevance  
- High practical value  

**Why not**
- Behavioural adoption required  
- Data sensitivity  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High

`adhd`, `stability`, `habits`, `tracking`
