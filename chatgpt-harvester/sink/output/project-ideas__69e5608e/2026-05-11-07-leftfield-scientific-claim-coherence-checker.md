# 7. Leftfield: “Scientific Claim Coherence Checker”


**Problem**  
Scientific and technical claims evolve, and inconsistencies emerge across papers, articles, and summaries.

**Approaches**
- Track claims across sources  
- Detect contradictions or shifts  
- Visualise coherence over time  

**Tech hints**
- Knowledge graphs  
- Semantic similarity models  
- Temporal linking  

**Formats**
- Research tool  
- Dashboard  
- API  

**Why good fit**
- Highly differentiated  
- Useful in research and media  

**Why not**
- Data ingestion complexity  
- Ambiguity in claims  

**Revenue potential**  
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium

`science`, `analysis`, `knowledge`, `tracking`
