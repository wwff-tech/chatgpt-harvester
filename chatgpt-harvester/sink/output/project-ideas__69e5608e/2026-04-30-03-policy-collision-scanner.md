## 3. Policy Collision Scanner


**Problem**  
Security, compliance, and operational policies can conflict or override each other in unintended ways.

**Approaches**
- Analyse policy sets for contradictions  
- Simulate policy execution paths  
- Highlight conflicting rules  

**Tech hints**
- Policy-as-code (e.g., Open Policy Agent)  
- Static analysis + rule evaluation  
- Graph modelling of policy relationships  

**Formats**
- CLI tool  
- CI/CD validation step  
- Web dashboard  

**Why good fit**
- Strong security alignment  
- Practical and implementable  

**Why not**
- Complex policy interactions  
- Requires structured inputs  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium → High  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium

`policy`, `security`, `compliance`, `analysis`
