# 1. Constraint Discovery Engine


## Problem Statement

Teams typically know:

- what they want,
- what they built,
- what failed.

They rarely know:

> What actually limits progress?

The bottleneck may be:

- approvals,
- dependencies,
- context switching,
- queue depth,
- ownership,
- permissions,
- organisational structure.

Most metrics systems reveal symptoms, not constraints.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Continuously analyse:

- incidents,
- tickets,
- deployments,
- telemetry,

to identify recurring limiting factors.

### Approach B

Apply Theory of Constraints style modelling across technical systems and workflows.

Rank bottlenecks by overall impact.
