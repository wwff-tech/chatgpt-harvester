# 7. Leftfield: “Supply Chain Accountability Tracker”


**Problem**  
Supply chains lack transparency in responsibility:
- unclear accountability for failures,  
- difficult tracing of issues across vendors.

**Approaches**
- Map supply chain entities and responsibilities  
- Track accountability for events  
- Enable traceability  

**Tech hints**
- Distributed ledgers or verifiable logs  
- Graph modelling  
- Integration with logistics data  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- API platform  
- Audit tool  

**Why good fit**
- High global relevance  
- Regulatory interest  

**Why not**
- Data sharing challenges  
- Coordination complexity  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium → High  

**Content potential**  
High  

**Community potential**  
Medium
