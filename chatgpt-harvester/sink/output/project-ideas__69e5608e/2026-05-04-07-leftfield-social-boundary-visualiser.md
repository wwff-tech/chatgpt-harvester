## 7. Leftfield: “Social Boundary Visualiser”


**Problem**  
Social and organisational boundaries (who owns what, who interacts with whom) are implicit and often unclear.

**Approaches**
- Map communication and collaboration patterns  
- Visualise boundaries and overlaps  
- Identify boundary violations or gaps  

**Tech hints**
- Graph analysis (network graphs)  
- Communication data (Slack, email metadata)  
- Clustering algorithms  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- CLI analyser  
- Organisational tool  

**Why good fit**
- Unique perspective on organisational health  
- Bridges technical and social systems  

**Why not**
- Privacy concerns  
- Requires sensitive data  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium
