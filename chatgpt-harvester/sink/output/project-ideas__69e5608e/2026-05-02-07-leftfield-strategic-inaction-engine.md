## 7. Leftfield: “Strategic Inaction Engine”


**Problem**  
Systems optimise for action, not for *deliberate non-action*, even when waiting would produce better outcomes.

**Approaches**
- Detect scenarios where inaction is optimal  
- Model cost of action vs waiting  
- Recommend delays or deferrals  

**Tech hints**
- Decision theory models  
- Cost-benefit analysis  
- Time-based simulation  

**Formats**
- Decision support tool  
- CLI analyser  
- Agent plugin  

**Why good fit**
- Novel and intellectually rigorous  
- Applies across domains  

**Why not**
- Hard to quantify benefits  
- Counterintuitive to users  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- Medium

`decision-making`, `strategy`, `analysis`, `systems`
