## 6. Attention Allocation Tracker


**Problem**  
People allocate attention reactively rather than strategically, leading to suboptimal outcomes.

**Approaches**
- Track attention distribution across tasks  
- Compare against intended priorities  
- Suggest reallocation  

**Tech hints**
- Time tracking + categorisation  
- Visual analytics (pie charts, timelines)  
- Lightweight ML for pattern detection  

**Formats**
- Desktop/mobile app  
- CLI + reports  
- Weekly summaries  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD/self-optimisation relevance  
- Visual and actionable  

**Why not**
- Requires consistent tracking  
- Behaviour change barrier  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High
