# 7. Leftfield: Delegation Market


## Problem Statement

Most organisations manage:

- work allocation,
- staffing,
- automation,
- outsourcing,

using separate systems.

They rarely optimise:

> Who is the best delegate for this task?

Whether the delegate is:

- human,
- AI,
- automation,
- contractor,
- specialised agent.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Build a marketplace for capabilities.

Match tasks to delegates based on:

- skill,
- confidence,
- availability,
- historical performance.

### Approach B

Create delegation simulations.

Predict outcomes before assigning work.
