# 6. Benchmark Builder for Real Systems


## Problem Statement

Many AI evaluations measure:

- toy problems,
- isolated tasks,
- artificial environments.

Real systems involve:

- ambiguity,
- noisy telemetry,
- partial failures,
- human constraints.

Research groups are increasingly building realistic operational benchmarks because existing evaluations miss critical failure modes. 

## Approaches

### Approach A

Create benchmark generators from production architecture patterns.

### Approach B

Generate realistic fault-injection environments automatically.

Measure performance under realistic operating conditions.
