## 3. “Policy Drift Visualiser”


**Problem**  
Security and compliance policies evolve, but understanding *how* they changed and their impact is difficult.

**Approaches**
- Version and diff policies over time  
- Visualise changes (permissions added/removed)  
- Highlight risk deltas  

**Tech hints**
- Git-based versioning  
- JSON/YAML diffing  
- Graph visualisation (permissions relationships)  

**Formats**
- Web dashboard  
- CLI tool  
- CI policy check  

**Why good fit**
- Strong security alignment  
- Increasing regulatory pressure  

**Why not**
- Requires structured policy definitions  
- Visualisation complexity  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium → High  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium
