# 3. “Adaptive Decision Policy Engine”


**Problem**  
Decision logic is often static (hardcoded thresholds, rules), but environments are dynamic.

**Approaches**
- Learn policies from historical decisions + outcomes  
- Adapt thresholds dynamically  
- Allow human override and constraints  

**Tech hints**
- Policy-as-code + learning layer  
- Reinforcement learning or bandits  
- Integration with systems like entity["software","Open Policy Agent","policy engine"]  

**Formats**
- Policy engine service  
- SDK  
- CI/CD enforcement layer  

**Why good fit**
- Moves systems from static → adaptive  
- Strong infra + AI crossover  

**Why not**
- Risk of unpredictable behaviour  
- Requires guardrails

High

High
