## 4. “Context Switching Heatmap” (Where Your Brain Actually Goes)


**Problem**  
People underestimate how fragmented their attention is throughout the day.

**Approaches**
- Track app/window focus changes  
- Build temporal heatmap of attention  
- Identify fragmentation patterns  

**Tech hints**
- OS-level hooks (cross-platform abstraction)  
- Time-series storage  
- Visualisation (heatmaps)  

**Formats**
- Desktop app  
- CLI + HTML report  
- Weekly summaries  

**Why good fit**
- Strong ADHD relevance  
- Visual, immediately actionable output  

**Why not**
- Privacy concerns (local-first needed)  
- Behaviour change not guaranteed  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- High

`attention`, `adhd`, `productivity`, `tracking`
