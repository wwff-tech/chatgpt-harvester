# 3. Operational Friction Mapper


## Problem Statement

Engineering organisations spend substantial effort fighting friction:

- repeated approvals,
- handovers,
- duplicated work,
- ticket churn,
- coordination overhead.

The friction is usually invisible until someone investigates manually.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Build friction heatmaps from:

- ticket systems,
- Git repositories,
- deployment pipelines,
- incident management systems.

### Approach B

Identify loops and rework cycles automatically.

Surface high-cost organisational paths.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Process mining
- Graph analytics
- Temporal databases
- Event sourcing
- Data warehouse integrations
