# 5. Activation Recipe Builder


## Problem Statement

ADHD productivity discussions increasingly emphasise:

- activation,
- environmental cues,
- body doubling,
- transition management,

rather than planning systems alone. 

Many individuals know *what* to do but struggle to initiate.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Track successful activation events.

Capture:

- location,
- time,
- sensory conditions,
- social context,
- task characteristics.

### Approach B

Generate personalised activation recipes.

Example:

> 08:30, headphones on, coffee prepared, virtual body-double session, first task under 10 minutes.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- local-first storage
- wearable integrations
- behavioural analytics
- pattern mining
- privacy-preserving ML

## Suggested Formats

- desktop utility
- mobile app
- Obsidian plugin
- Raycast extension

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong ADHD relevance
- Behaviour-centric rather than task-centric
- Personal experimentation appeal

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Behavioural modelling is noisy
- Requires longitudinal data
