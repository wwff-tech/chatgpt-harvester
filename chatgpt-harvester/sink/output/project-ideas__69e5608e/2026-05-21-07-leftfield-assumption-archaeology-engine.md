# 7. Leftfield: Assumption Archaeology Engine


## Problem Statement

Complex systems accumulate hidden assumptions.

Nobody remembers:

- why a timeout exists,
- why a retry count is 7,
- why a process requires approval,
- why a policy was introduced.

Eventually assumptions become cargo cults.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Mine assumptions from:

- code,
- tickets,
- runbooks,
- postmortems.

### Approach B

Track assumption validity continuously.

Flag assumptions no longer supported by evidence.
