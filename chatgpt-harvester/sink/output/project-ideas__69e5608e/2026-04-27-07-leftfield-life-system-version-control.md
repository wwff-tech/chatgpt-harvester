## 7. Leftfield: “Life System Version Control”


**Problem**  
People change habits, routines, and systems without tracking what changed or why outcomes differ.

**Approaches**
- Version personal systems (routines, habits, workflows)  
- Track changes and outcomes  
- Enable rollback or experimentation  

**Tech hints**
- Git-like model for structured data  
- Change tracking + tagging  
- Simple analytics layer  

**Formats**
- CLI + local storage  
- Web UI  
- Notebook-style interface  

**Why good fit**
- Strong systems-thinking alignment  
- Unique framing of self-improvement  

**Why not**
- Requires disciplined input  
- Abstract for many users  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- Medium

`self-improvement`, `systems`, `tracking`, `experimentation`
