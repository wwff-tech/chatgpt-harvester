# 7. Leftfield: “Historical Decision Analyzer”


**Problem**  
Historical events are analysed narratively, not systematically in terms of decision quality.

**Approaches**
- Model decisions in historical contexts  
- Evaluate outcomes vs alternatives  
- Identify patterns in decision-making  

**Tech hints**
- Knowledge graphs  
- Counterfactual modelling  
- Data aggregation  

**Formats**
- Research tool  
- Educational platform  
- Interactive timeline  

**Why good fit**
- Unique and intellectually rich  
- Cross-disciplinary appeal  

**Why not**
- Speculative modelling  
- Data ambiguity  

**Revenue potential**  
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium  

**Tags**  
`history`, `analysis`, `decisions`, `research`
