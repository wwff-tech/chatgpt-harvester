# 7. Leftfield: Scarcity Simulator


## Problem Statement

Most planning tools assume:

- abundant resources,
- stable supply,
- predictable conditions.

Real systems increasingly face scarcity:

- compute,
- energy,
- talent,
- attention,
- water,
- critical materials.

Few tools help explore systemic effects of resource scarcity.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Agent-based simulation of constrained ecosystems.

### Approach B

Model cascading effects when key resources become limited.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Agent-based modelling
- System dynamics
- Graph simulation
- Public datasets
- Scenario planning frameworks

## Suggested Formats

- Research platform
- Educational simulator
- Strategic planning tool

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Strong systems-thinking appeal

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit
