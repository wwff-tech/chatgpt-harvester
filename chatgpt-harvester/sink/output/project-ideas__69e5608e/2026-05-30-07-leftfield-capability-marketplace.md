# 7. Leftfield: Capability Marketplace


## Problem Statement

The emerging stack includes:

- humans,
- specialised agents,
- MCP tools,
- automation systems,
- external services.

Most organisations allocate work poorly because capability discovery is weak.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Build a searchable capability graph.

Answer:

> Who or what is best suited for this task?

### Approach B

Route work dynamically based on:

- confidence,
- cost,
- availability,
- historical performance.
