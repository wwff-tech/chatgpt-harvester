## 7. Leftfield: “Narrative Continuity Tracker”


**Problem**  
Stories, explanations, or public narratives shift over time without clear tracking of changes.

**Approaches**
- Track narrative changes across sources  
- Detect inconsistencies or reversals  
- Visualise narrative evolution  

**Tech hints**
- NLP + embedding comparison  
- Timeline visualisation  
- Source aggregation  

**Formats**
- Dashboard  
- CLI analyser  
- Research tool  

**Why good fit**
- Unique and intellectually rich  
- Applies to media, AI, and communication  

**Why not**
- Subjective interpretation  
- Risk of misuse  

**Revenue potential**
- Low → Medium  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- Medium
