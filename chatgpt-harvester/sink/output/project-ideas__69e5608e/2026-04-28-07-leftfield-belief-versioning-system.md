## 7. Leftfield: “Belief Versioning System”


**Problem**  
People update beliefs over time but rarely track *why* they changed, losing reasoning context.

**Approaches**
- Version beliefs with supporting evidence  
- Track changes and triggers  
- Enable reflection and rollback  

**Tech hints**
- Structured data model (belief + evidence + timestamp)  
- Change tracking  
- Lightweight graph relationships  

**Formats**
- CLI tool  
- Notebook-style interface  
- Personal knowledge system plugin  

**Why good fit**
- Strong alignment with analytical thinking  
- Unique framing  

**Why not**
- Requires disciplined use  
- Niche appeal  

**Revenue potential**
- Low  

**Content potential**
- Very high  

**Community potential**
- Medium
