## 3. “Implicit Contract Detector”


**Problem**  
Systems depend on undocumented assumptions (API shapes, timing, formats), leading to fragile integrations.

**Approaches**
- Analyse runtime interactions  
- Infer contracts (schemas, expectations)  
- Detect deviations  

**Tech hints**
- Traffic capture (API logs)  
- Schema inference  
- Diffing over time  

**Formats**
- CLI tool  
- CI validation step  
- Dashboard  

**Why good fit**
- Strong distributed systems alignment  
- Surfaces hidden complexity  

**Why not**
- Inference accuracy varies  
- Requires sufficient traffic  

**Revenue potential**
- Medium → High  

**Content potential**
- High  

**Community potential**
- Medium
