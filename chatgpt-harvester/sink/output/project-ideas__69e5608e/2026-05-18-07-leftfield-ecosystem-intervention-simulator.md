# 7. Leftfield: “Ecosystem Intervention Simulator”


**Problem**  
Interventions in complex ecosystems (environmental, economic) often have unintended global consequences.

**Approaches**
- Model ecosystem interactions  
- Simulate interventions  
- Visualise ripple effects  

**Tech hints**
- System dynamics modelling  
- Agent-based simulations  
- Public datasets  

**Formats**
- Simulation tool  
- Educational platform  
- Research dashboard  

**Why good fit**
- High intellectual and societal value  
- Cross-disciplinary appeal  

**Why not**
- Complex modelling  
- Data limitations  

**Revenue potential**  
Low → Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
Medium
