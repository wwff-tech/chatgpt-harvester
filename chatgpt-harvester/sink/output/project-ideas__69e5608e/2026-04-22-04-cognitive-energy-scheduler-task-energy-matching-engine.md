## 4. “Cognitive Energy Scheduler” (Task → Energy Matching Engine)


**Problem**  
People (especially with ADHD/ASD traits) schedule tasks by time, not by cognitive energy or context.

**Approaches**
- Tag tasks with energy profiles (deep focus, admin, creative)
- Track user energy patterns over time
- Suggest optimal task sequencing dynamically

**Tech hints**
- Simple rules engine first
- Time-series tracking (SQLite/DuckDB)
- Optional ML later

**Formats**
- CLI + web UI
- Daily planner generator
- Integration with calendar

**Why good fit**
- Direct personal relevance
- Combines structured thinking with behavioural insight

**Why not**
- Requires consistent input to be useful
- Hard to model energy accurately

**Revenue potential**
- Medium

**Content potential**
- High

**Community potential**
- High
