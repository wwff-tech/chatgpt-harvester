# 6. Cognitive Load Pruner


**Problem**  
People accumulate:
- tasks,  
- commitments,  
- tools.

Leading to overload and reduced effectiveness, especially in ADHD/ASD contexts.

**Approaches**
- Track commitments and usage  
- Identify low-value or redundant items  
- Suggest pruning strategies  

**Tech hints**
- Activity tracking  
- Frequency analysis  
- Local-first data models  

**Formats**
- Desktop app  
- CLI tool  
- Weekly report generator  

**Why good fit**
- Strong personal relevance  
- Clear productivity benefits  

**Why not**
- Behavioural adoption required  
- Subjective value judgements  

**Revenue potential**  
Medium  

**Content potential**  
Very high  

**Community potential**  
High
