# 2. Approval Budget Manager


## Problem Statement

Current governance systems tend to be binary:

- fully autonomous,
- fully human-approved.

Recent governance discussions suggest this model is failing. 

Many workflows need:

- limited autonomy,
- bounded approval rights,
- dynamic oversight.

## Approaches

### Approach A

Treat approvals as a consumable budget.

Agents spend approval tokens based on:

- risk,
- impact,
- confidence.

### Approach B

Dynamically adjust approval requirements using:

- historical performance,
- confidence,
- operational context.

## Suggested Tech Hints

- Policy engines
- Risk scoring
- Bayesian confidence models
- Open Policy Agent
- Workflow engines

## Suggested Formats

- Agent governance platform
- CI/CD extension
- Internal developer platform feature

## Why It Is A Good Fit

- Solves a real governance gap
- Compatible with existing workflows
- Clear enterprise relevance

## Why It Is Not A Good Fit

- Hard to model risk correctly
- Organisational adoption challenges
