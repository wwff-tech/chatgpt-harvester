### 9. Left-field — “The Most Important Property of a Test Lab Is That Reality Cannot Reach Back Through It”


The OpenAI incident has a broader analogue in engineering laboratories. 

**Angle:** We tend to define isolation in one direction:

> production must not contaminate the test.

For adversarial or autonomous systems, the more important direction may be:

> **the test must not contaminate production.**

Malware sandboxes, chemical fume cupboards, explosive test cells, biological containment, RF anechoic chambers, and autonomous-agent environments all share this property.

The experiment may behave in ways the experimenter did not predict; **containment exists precisely because prediction has failed**.

That's an excellent principle for agent infrastructure:

> If your sandbox depends on the agent behaving as expected, it isn't a sandbox.
