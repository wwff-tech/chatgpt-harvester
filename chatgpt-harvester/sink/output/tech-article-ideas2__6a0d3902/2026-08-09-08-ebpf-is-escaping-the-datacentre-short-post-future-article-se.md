### 8. **“eBPF Is Escaping the Datacentre”** — Short post / future article seed


The eBPF Foundation recently added Toyota alongside Meta, while its research programme explicitly calls out embedded systems, hardware acceleration, end-host networking, high-performance networking, and hardware fault isolation as areas of interest. 

**Angle:** We've mentally filed eBPF under “cloud-native Linux”, but automotive and embedded adoption changes the design space considerably: harder real-time constraints, constrained hardware, long product lifetimes, safety requirements, and much less tolerance for “just update the kernel”.

**Matches:** Linux, embedded systems, ESP32-adjacent thinking, networking, observability.
