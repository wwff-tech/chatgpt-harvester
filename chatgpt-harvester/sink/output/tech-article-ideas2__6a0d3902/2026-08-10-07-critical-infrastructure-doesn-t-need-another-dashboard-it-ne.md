### 7. Critical Infrastructure Doesn't Need Another Dashboard. It Needs Someone to Actually Fix Things.


Today, DEF CON's Franklin project announced expansion of its programme protecting small US water utilities. The interesting design isn't merely volunteer cybersecurity: a new Water Watch Center will coordinate managed security providers, threat intelligence, and volunteers so alerts can turn into remediation. The problem is enormous — roughly 150,000 water organisations, with 98% characterised as small businesses. 

**Angle:** The project's insight is wonderfully SRE-like:

> **Detection without a delivery mechanism is not a security capability.**

Small organisations don't primarily lack another threat feed. They lack people capable of turning `CVE → affected? → mitigation → implementation → validation`.

This generalises well to open-source projects, charities, schools, local government, small businesses, and home users.

**Matches:** SRE, incident response, critical infrastructure, security operations, organisational design.
