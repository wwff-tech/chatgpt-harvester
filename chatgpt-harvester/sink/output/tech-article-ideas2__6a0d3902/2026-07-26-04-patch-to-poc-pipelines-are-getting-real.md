## 4) Patch-to-PoC Pipelines Are Getting Real


**Suggested title:** *Publishing a Patch Now Starts the Clock*

**Summary:**  
Research on **K-Repro** demonstrates that agentic AI systems can generate proof-of-concept exploits directly from Linux kernel security patches for a substantial proportion of historical vulnerabilities. The practical effect is a shrinking window between disclosure and exploit availability.
