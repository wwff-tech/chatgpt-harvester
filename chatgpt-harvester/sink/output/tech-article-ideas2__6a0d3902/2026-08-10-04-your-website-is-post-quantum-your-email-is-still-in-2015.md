### 4. Your Website Is Post-Quantum. Your Email Is Still in 2015.


The same UK research gives a lovely compact statistic: **44% PQC support on HTTPS, 6.4% on SMTP**, a roughly sevenfold difference. 

**Angle:** Protocol modernisation follows **ownership boundaries**, not merely technical feasibility.

Web front doors increasingly terminate at managed CDNs and cloud load balancers. SMTP commonly traverses much older and more heterogeneous infrastructure.

The useful question isn't “why is email bad?” but:

**“Which infrastructure improves automatically when vendors innovate, and which infrastructure requires every organisation to migrate itself?”**

That distinction predicts technological stagnation surprisingly well.

**Matches:** email infrastructure, DMARC/TLS, cloud, legacy systems, SRE.

**Confidence: 0.95.**
