## 5) Embedded Linux Has a Supply-Chain Problem, Not a Patching Problem


**Suggested title:** *Your Router Isn't Old Because the Vendor Forgot*

**Summary:**  
Recent research shows many SOHO devices inherit obsolete Linux kernels through SoC vendor SDKs, creating "kernel lock-in". OEMs often cannot realistically upgrade without vendor cooperation, propagating vulnerability debt throughout the supply chain. 

**Angle:**  
Challenge the simplistic "manufacturers don't update firmware" narrative by examining the structural causes.

**Matches your interests:** Embedded Linux, electronics, supply-chain security

**Format:** **Long-form article**

**Suggested points**
- SoC SDK dependency
- OEM versus ODM responsibilities
- Long-term support economics
- Community firmware projects
- Regulatory approaches
