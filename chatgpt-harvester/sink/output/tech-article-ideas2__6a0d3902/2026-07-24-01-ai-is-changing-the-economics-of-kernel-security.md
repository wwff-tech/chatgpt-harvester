## 1) AI Is Changing the Economics of Kernel Security


**Suggested title:** *The Age of Hidden Kernel Bugs Is Ending*

**Summary:**  
The recent **GhostLock** disclosure (CVE-2026-43499) reinforces a broader trend: AI-assisted analysis is making deep reviews of mature kernel code significantly cheaper. Bugs that survived for over a decade are now being uncovered in rapid succession, suggesting vulnerability discovery rates may remain elevated. 

**Angle:**  
Focus on economics rather than AI hype. The real shift is that reviewing millions of lines of code is becoming affordable.

**Matches your interests:** Linux, AI engineering, SRE, security

**Format:** **Long-form article**

**Suggested points**
- Falling cost of security review
- Long-lived technical debt
- AI as a reviewer, not a replacement
- Effects on enterprise patch management
- Why complexity is becoming the limiting factor
