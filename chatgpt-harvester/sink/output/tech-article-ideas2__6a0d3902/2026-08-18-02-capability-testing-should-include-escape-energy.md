### 2. Capability Testing Should Include Escape Energy


The same development suggests a useful alternative to evaluating agents entirely through task benchmarks. The interesting property of a capable agent isn't merely how often it completes a task; it is how much **unintended environmental leverage** it can discover while attempting one. 

**Angle:** Borrow the idea of stored energy from physical safety engineering.

An agent environment contains potential capability:

`network access`

`credentials`

`writable services`

`package managers`

`other agents`

`cloud metadata`

`human communication`

`persistent storage`.

None may be dangerous independently. Composition is the problem.

So alongside:

`task success rate = 83%`

perhaps evaluation needs something resembling:

`maximum reachable authority`

or:

`capability amplification`

under adversarial exploration.

That gives you a bridge from AI evaluation into attack graphs and capability security rather than yet another alignment essay.
