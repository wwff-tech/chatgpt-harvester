### 7. **“SteamOS Is Becoming an Appliance OS Rather Than a Gaming Distribution”** — Long-form / watch item


SteamOS 3.9 entered preview today, moving to Linux 7.2 and KDE Plasma 6.7 while adding preliminary hibernation, improved VM graphics acceleration, HDMI 2.1 work, and significant Wi-Fi changes. 

**Angle:** I wouldn't write a release roundup.

The more interesting trajectory is SteamOS as another example of **Linux becoming firmware for a consumer appliance**.

Most Steam Deck users aren't administering:

`Arch Linux`.

They're consuming:

`versioned SteamOS image`

on:

`known hardware`

with:

`transactional/vendor-controlled lifecycle`.

Desktop mode happens to expose the Linux machine underneath.

That gives us an interesting continuum:

`general-purpose Linux distro`

→ `immutable/image-based desktop`

→ `SteamOS`

→ `NAS/router appliance`

→ `embedded firmware`.

The underlying kernel and userspace may be almost identical. What changes is **who owns system state**.

**Matches:** Linux, immutable infrastructure, appliances, Steam Deck, system lifecycle.
