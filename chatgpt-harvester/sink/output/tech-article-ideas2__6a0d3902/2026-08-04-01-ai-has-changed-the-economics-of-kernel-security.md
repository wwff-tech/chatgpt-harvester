## 1) AI Has Changed the Economics of Kernel Security


**Suggested title:** *The Cheapest Part of Security Is Now Finding the Bug*

**Summary**  
The **GhostLock** Linux kernel vulnerability (CVE-2026-43499) remained hidden for around 15 years before being uncovered with AI-assisted analysis. The real story is not the CVE itself, but that reviewing mature codebases is becoming dramatically cheaper. 

**Angle**

Frame this as an economics shift. AI is reducing discovery costs, which means verification, patch rollout, and operational readiness become the new bottlenecks.
