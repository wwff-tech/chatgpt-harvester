## 2) Kernel CVEs Are Becoming More Operationally Significant


**Suggested title:** *The Linux Kernel Is Becoming Its Own Security Feed*

**Summary:**  
The Linux kernel has recently begun publishing hundreds of CVEs directly through its own CVE authority. This doesn't necessarily mean Linux suddenly became less secure—it changes how vulnerabilities are tracked and communicated. Community discussion suggests large publication bursts can be tied to routine stable releases.

Explain why changes in disclosure processes can create the illusion of worsening security.
