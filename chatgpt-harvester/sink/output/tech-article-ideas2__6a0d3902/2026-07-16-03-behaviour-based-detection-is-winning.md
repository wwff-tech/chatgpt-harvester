## 3) Behaviour-Based Detection Is Winning


- **Title:** *Stop Looking for CVEs. Start Looking for Behaviour.*
- **Summary:** Recent eBPF detection research focuses on identifying exploit behaviour rather than matching individual vulnerabilities such as Copy Fail or Dirty Frag. That approach is likely to age better as exploit techniques evolve. 
- **Angle:** Detection engineering is gradually moving from signatures to behavioural analytics.
