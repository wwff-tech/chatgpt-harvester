### 6. Use AI to Discover the State Machine, Then Throw the AI Away


The QuEra result has an excellent compact form. 

**Angle:** There are two fundamentally different ways to automate something poorly understood:

`LLM observes → decides → acts → repeat forever`

or:

`LLM explores → discovers policy → generates conventional controller → validate → deploy`.

The second gives you a finite artefact to reason about.

It can be:

`code reviewed`

`fuzzed`

`model checked`

`bench tested`

`version controlled`

`rolled back`.

That may be one of the most productive patterns for physical AI:

> **Use probabilistic systems to discover deterministic automation.**
