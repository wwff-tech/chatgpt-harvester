## 1) The Cost of Finding Linux Kernel Bugs Just Collapsed


- **Title:** *AI Is Making Decade-Old Kernel Bugs Look Brand New*
- **Summary:** The disclosure of **GhostLock** (CVE-2026-43499), a privilege-escalation vulnerability introduced in 2011 and reportedly discovered with AI assistance, reinforces a broader trend: reviewing mature kernel code is becoming dramatically cheaper. Multiple vendors have issued urgent advisories and patches over the past fortnight. 
- **Angle:** This isn't an "AI hacks Linux" story. It's an engineering economics story: defenders and attackers both benefit when review costs fall.
