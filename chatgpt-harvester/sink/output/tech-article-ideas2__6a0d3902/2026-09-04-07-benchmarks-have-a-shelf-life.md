## 7. Benchmarks Have a Shelf Life


OpenAI notes that Astra saturated earlier exploit benchmarks and required new evaluation methodologies. Similar issues are emerging across coding and cyber evaluations. 

**Angle**

Every benchmark eventually becomes:

- training data
- optimisation target
- regression suite
- marketing metric

At that point it measures historical competence rather than frontier capability.

That argues for continuously refreshed evaluation sets with unknown tasks rather than permanent public leaderboards.

The same applies to:

- coding
- autonomous agents
- vulnerability discovery
- robotics
