## 7) AI Bug Hunting Creates a New Bottleneck


**Suggested title:** *When Success Creates More Work*

**Summary:**  
If AI continues increasing vulnerability discovery rates, maintainers will spend proportionally more time validating reports, confirming exploitability, coordinating disclosures, and reviewing fixes. Automation doesn't remove bottlenecks—it relocates them.
