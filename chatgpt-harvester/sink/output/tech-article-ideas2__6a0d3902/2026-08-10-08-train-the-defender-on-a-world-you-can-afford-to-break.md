### 8. Train the Defender on a World You Can Afford to Break


The same project is partnering with Vanderbilt using research from DARPA's CASTLE programme to construct **digital twins of water-treatment environments**. Red- and blue-team agents can repeatedly attack and defend the simulated infrastructure, with the eventual ambition of deploying defensive automation to real facilities. 

**Angle:** This is one of the rare agentic-AI ideas where the safety architecture is more interesting than the model.

Don't let an AI learn by experimenting on a sewage plant.

Give it a high-fidelity world in which:

- failure is cheap,
- attacks are reproducible,
- ground truth exists,
- experiments can run thousands of times,
- successful policies can be tested against unseen scenarios,
- and deployment remains gated.

That's essentially **reinforcement learning crossed with chaos engineering crossed with a staging environment**.

It also suggests an intriguing future for infrastructure engineering: digital twins might eventually become *the test suite for physical systems*.

**Matches:** agentic AI, digital twins, chaos engineering, industrial control, robotics, simulation.
