## 3) "Local Privilege Escalation" No Longer Means What Most People Think


**Suggested title:** *Every Container Makes Local Root a Platform Problem*

**Summary:**  
Ubuntu advisories continue to highlight vulnerabilities such as Copy Fail, Dirty Frag, and Fragnesia because "local" attackers often already exist inside CI runners, Kubernetes clusters, or multi-tenant infrastructure. Container escape transforms a local flaw into a platform-wide concern. 

**Angle:**  
Challenge outdated mental models that treat local privilege escalation as low priority.

**Matches your interests:** Kubernetes, Linux, SRE, cloud security

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points**

- Shared-kernel architecture
- CI/CD runners
- Container escape
- Exposure-based prioritisation
- Runtime mitigations
