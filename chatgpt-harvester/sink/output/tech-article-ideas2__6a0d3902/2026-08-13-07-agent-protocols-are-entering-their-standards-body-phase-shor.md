### 7. **“Agent Protocols Are Entering Their Standards-Body Phase”** — Short post / watch item


The Agentic AI Foundation announced today that it has added **57 organisations**, bringing reported membership to 247. The important signal isn't the membership number itself, but that agent interoperability is moving from individual vendor protocols towards institutional governance. 

**Angle:** Technologies tend to follow a recognisable lifecycle:

`experiment → popular implementation → competing implementations → interoperability pain → standards politics`

The agent ecosystem has reached the last two stages remarkably quickly.

The article becomes interesting if you compare it with HTTP, OCI, Kubernetes CRI/CNI/CSI, OpenTelemetry, and OAuth: standards often emerge **after enough people have accumulated incompatible working systems**.

I'd hold this to a short post unless something substantive emerges around protocol convergence.
