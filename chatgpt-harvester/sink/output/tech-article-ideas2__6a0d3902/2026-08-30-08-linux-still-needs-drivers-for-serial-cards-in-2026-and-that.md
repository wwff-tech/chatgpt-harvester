### 8. **“Linux Still Needs Drivers for Serial Cards in 2026, and That's a Feature”** — Short / hardware post


Linux 7.3 today gained a new driver for Moxa PCIe multi-port serial boards, supporting hardware exposing **up to 32 serial ports**. Other Linux 7.3 platform-driver work landing today includes additional AMD Zen 6 preparation and support around current Ryzen AI hardware. 

**Angle:** Skip the Zen 6 speculation. Write about serial ports.

Modern software discourse imagines:

`HTTP`

`gRPC`

`Kubernetes`

`cloud APIs`.

The physical world remains full of:

`RS-232`

`RS-422`

`RS-485`

`Modbus`

`console ports`

`CNC machines`

`industrial instruments`.

A 32-port serial card getting a new upstream Linux driver in 2026 is a nice reminder that **boring interfaces acquire enormous longevity precisely because they are boring**.

RS-232 dates to 1960. You can still buy new enterprise hardware for it 66 years later.

Compare that with the expected lifetime of today's SaaS APIs.
