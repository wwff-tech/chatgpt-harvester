## 1) AI Is Permanently Changing Linux Security


**Suggested title:** *When Code Review Stops Being the Bottleneck*

**Summary:**  
The discovery of the 15-year-old **GhostLock** vulnerability demonstrates that AI-assisted review can uncover subtle flaws that survived more than a decade of human scrutiny. The significance isn't one bug—it's that the cost of reviewing millions of lines of mature code is falling rapidly. 

**Angle**

Treat this as an economics story, not an AI story. If bug discovery becomes cheap, remediation, testing, and rollout become the scarce resources.
