## 5) eBPF Is Becoming Linux's Runtime Platform


**Suggested title:** *The Most Important Runtime You've Probably Never Designed For*

**Summary:**  
Research continues to reinforce that eBPF has evolved from packet filtering into a mature runtime capable of extending networking, observability, security, and even kernel behaviour. At the same time, proposals such as SafeBPF show that protecting eBPF itself is becoming important. 

**Angle:**  
Describe eBPF as Linux's programmable substrate rather than another kernel subsystem.

**Matches your interests:** Linux, observability, networking, cloud-native

**Format:** **Long-form article**

**Suggested points**

- Runtime programmability
- Cilium and networking
- Runtime security
- SafeBPF
- Future kernel architecture
