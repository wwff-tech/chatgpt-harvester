### 7. The Open Hardware Supply Chain Has a Closed-Source Dependency Called Economics


The Pine64 announcement provides a crisp companion argument. 

**Angle:** Open-source advocates naturally think about dependency freedom in terms of licences and source availability.

Hardware has another dependency graph:

`design`

→ `fabrication`

→ `packaging`

→ `DRAM`

→ `flash`

→ `connectors`

→ `power ICs`

→ `shipping`.

You can have source for everything above the silicon and still be utterly dependent on three factories thousands of miles away.

The physical-world equivalent of `apt source` doesn't exist.
