- **Title:** *What If the Linux Kernel Has Become Too Large to Secure?*
- **Summary:** Recent disclosures including Copy Fail, CIFSwitch, and Fragnesia reveal a pattern of privilege-escalation vulnerabilities surviving for years in mature subsystems. Several affect cloud, Kubernetes, and CI/CD environments directly. 
- **Angle:** Examine whether Linux security is becoming a problem of cognitive scale rather than engineering quality.
- **Matches Interests:** Linux, security, SRE, systems engineering

- Why bugs survive for a decade or more
- Security review limits

- Complexity as a vulnerability class
- Implications for operators
