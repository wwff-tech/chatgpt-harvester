## 1) The Economics of Linux Security Have Changed


**Suggested title:** *When Finding Bugs Becomes Cheaper Than Fixing Them*

**Summary:**  
Recent disclosures such as **GhostLock (CVE-2026-43499)**, alongside earlier issues like Copy Fail, suggest we're entering an era where AI-assisted analysis dramatically lowers the cost of discovering deep kernel flaws. The bottleneck is shifting from discovery to remediation. 

**Angle:**  
Rather than discussing "AI hackers", explore the economic consequences. If every major codebase becomes significantly cheaper to audit, vulnerability discovery rates may permanently increase.

**Matches your interests:** Linux, SRE, AI engineering, security, systems thinking

**Format:** **Long-form article**

**Suggested points**

- Falling cost of code review
- Discovery versus remediation economics
- Open-source maintainer workload
- Fleet patch management
- Why complexity becomes technical debt with interest
