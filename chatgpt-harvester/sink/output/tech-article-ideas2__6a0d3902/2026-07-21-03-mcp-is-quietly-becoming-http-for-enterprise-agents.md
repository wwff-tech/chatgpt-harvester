## 3) MCP Is Quietly Becoming HTTP for Enterprise Agents


**Suggested title:** *The Protocol Is Finished. The Platform Is Just Beginning.*

**Summary:**  
The upcoming MCP specification introduces a stateless core, improved OAuth/OpenID Connect alignment, extensions, and cache-friendly operation, making production deployment substantially easier. At the same time, vendors are launching dedicated MCP gateways for governance and policy enforcement. 

**Angle:**  
Compare today's MCP ecosystem to the early years of REST APIs: once the protocol stabilises, the surrounding infrastructure becomes the real differentiator.

**Matches your interests:** AI infrastructure, distributed systems, platform engineering

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points**
- Stateless transport
- Gateway patterns
- Authentication hardening
- Enterprise operations
- Why governance follows standardisation
