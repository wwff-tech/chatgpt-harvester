## 5) Agent Governance Needs an Architectural Layer Above MCP


**Suggested title:** *Protocols Coordinate. Governance Decides.*

**Summary:**  
New research argues that protocols such as MCP, A2A, and ACP successfully standardise communication, but do not provide primitives for organisational governance, voting, dissent, or escalation. Those capabilities belong in a higher architectural layer. 

**Angle:**  
Draw parallels with TCP/IP, Kubernetes, and OAuth: standards enable systems, but governance lives above them.

**Matches your interests:** Agent architecture, distributed systems, systems thinking

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points**

- Coordination versus governance
- Human approval paths
- Multi-agent decision making
- Policy engines
- Enterprise architecture
