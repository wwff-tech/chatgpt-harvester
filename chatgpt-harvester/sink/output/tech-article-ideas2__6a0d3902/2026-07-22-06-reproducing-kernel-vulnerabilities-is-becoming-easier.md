## 6) Reproducing Kernel Vulnerabilities Is Becoming Easier


**Suggested title:** *Testing Kernel Bugs Shouldn't Take Days Anymore*

**Summary:**  
Research projects such as **KernJC** automate creation of vulnerable Linux environments, identifying the correct kernel version and configuration needed to reproduce historical vulnerabilities. That makes validation and education substantially easier.
