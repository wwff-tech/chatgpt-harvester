### 9. Left-field — “Compatibility Is the Dark Matter of Software Engineering”


ReactOS provides today's deliberate detour. 

**Angle:** Software interfaces have two specifications.

The first is written:

`documentation`

`standards`

`headers`

`RFCs`.

The second is everything software has discovered it can get away with:

`bugs`

`timing`

`error codes`

`filesystem ordering`

`undefined behaviour`

`undocumented APIs`

`quirks`.

The second specification is invisible until somebody implements the first one correctly and discovers that applications still break.

That's why compatibility projects are scientifically interesting: Wine, ReactOS, Samba, browser engines, emulators, and alternative language runtimes are effectively **instruments for measuring undocumented software behaviour**.

They reveal the gravitational effects of things we can't see directly.
