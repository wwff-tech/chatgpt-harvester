### 8. **“RISC-V Servers Have Reached the Boring Hardware Stage”** — Short / watch item


Fresh reporting today on SiFive's BigSky SF-2U870 emphasises what makes the system significant: **32 P870-D cores, 256 GB DDR5, four PCIe 5.0 ×16 slots, standard NVMe/networking, and ordinary enterprise Linux support**, including Ubuntu 26.04 and RHEL 10. 

We touched BigSky briefly last week, so I wouldn't repeat the announcement itself.

**Angle:** The follow-up worth watching is **boring compatibility**.

RISC-V's next milestone isn't another exotic development board.

It's:

`standard 2U chassis`

`standard NIC`

`standard NVMe`

`standard GPU`

`standard distro`

`standard CI runner`.

Architecture adoption accelerates when engineers stop needing to care about the architecture.

The useful future article is therefore not “RISC-V gets faster”, but:

> **When can I add `riscv64` to CI and otherwise forget about it?**
