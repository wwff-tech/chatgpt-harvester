### 9. Left-field — “Infrastructure Is What Remains After the Novelty Wears Off”


The RISC-V and cloud-device stories point in opposite directions but towards the same phenomenon. Physical phones are becoming cloud resources, while exotic CPUs are becoming ordinary Linux machines. 

**Angle:** Infrastructure engineering is largely the art of **removing interesting properties from interesting technology**.

The progression is often:

`novel → bespoke → automated → standardised → interchangeable → boring`

Cloud made servers boring. Kubernetes made workload placement boring-ish. Let's not oversell that one. OCI made container runtimes interchangeable. ACPI/UEFI are helping make CPU architecture less operationally interesting.

And now device clouds are attempting the same trick with **physical state**.

The provocative conclusion:

> “Boring” is not an insult in infrastructure. It is what successful abstraction looks like.
