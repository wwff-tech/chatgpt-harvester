## 5) Embedded Linux Is Accumulating Security Debt Through Its Supply Chain


**Suggested title:** *Your Router Didn't Choose That Old Kernel*

**Summary:**  
Recent research traces vulnerable SOHO firmware back through SoC SDKs, showing how kernel lock-in propagates from chip vendors to ODMs, OEMs, and finally end users. The problem is structural rather than simply negligent patching. 

**Angle:**  
Reframe firmware security as a supply-chain governance problem.

**Matches your interests:** Embedded Linux, electronics, supply-chain security

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points to cover**

- SDK lock-in
- Vendor dependencies
- Regulatory approaches
- Open-source firmware
- Long-term support economics
