## 3) Confidential Computing Is Becoming Part of the AI Stack


**Suggested title:** *The Next Trust Boundary Lives Inside the CPU*

**Summary**  
Canonical is making the case that confidential computing is moving beyond niche workloads into regulated AI inference, healthcare, finance, and cross-organisational collaboration. Protecting *data in use* is becoming as important as protecting data at rest or in transit. 

**Angle**

Position confidential computing as the runtime equivalent of zero trust: reducing how much infrastructure must be trusted while workloads execute.
