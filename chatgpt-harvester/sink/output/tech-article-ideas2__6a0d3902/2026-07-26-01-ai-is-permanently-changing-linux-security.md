## 1) AI Is Permanently Changing Linux Security


**Suggested title:** *The Cost of Finding Kernel Bugs Just Collapsed*

**Summary:**  
The recent **GhostLock** disclosure, following **Copy Fail** and **Fragnesia**, reinforces a structural shift: AI-assisted review is making deep inspection of mature kernel code dramatically cheaper. That means the industry should expect a sustained increase in vulnerability discovery rather than a one-off spike. 

**Angle:**  
Treat this as an economics story rather than an AI story. When review costs fall, decades of accumulated security debt becomes visible.

**Matches your interests:** Linux, AI engineering, SRE, cloud security

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points to cover**

- AI-assisted auditing versus fuzzing
- Long-lived bugs in mature code
- Discovery cost versus remediation cost
- Enterprise patch cadence
- Why complexity becomes the dominant risk
