## 4) Incomplete Security Fixes May Be the Next Big Research Area


**Suggested title:** *The Patch Was Correct—Just Not Complete*

**Summary:**  
Recent academic work studies *incomplete* Linux kernel security fixes, showing that some patches fail to eliminate the original vulnerability completely or introduce new weaknesses. Detecting incomplete fixes may become just as important as detecting original bugs.
