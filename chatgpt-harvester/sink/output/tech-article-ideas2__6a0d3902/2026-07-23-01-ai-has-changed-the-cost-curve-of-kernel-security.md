## 1) AI Has Changed the Cost Curve of Kernel Security


**Suggested title:** *When Finding Bugs Becomes Cheaper Than Fixing Them*

**Summary:**  
The discovery of the 15-year-old **GhostLock** kernel vulnerability, alongside recent flaws such as Copy Fail and Fragnesia, highlights a structural change: AI-assisted analysis is making deep reviews of mature kernel code substantially cheaper. The likely outcome is not a one-off spike, but a sustained increase in vulnerability discovery. 

**Angle:**  
Treat this as an economics story rather than an AI story. Once review costs fall, security debt becomes much more visible.

**Matches your interests:** Linux, AI engineering, security, SRE

**Format:** **Long-form article**

**Suggested points**

- AI-assisted code review versus fuzzing
- Discovery cost versus remediation cost
- Why mature codebases are being re-examined
- Implications for enterprise patch cycles
- Maintaining confidence in large codebases
