## 4) Agent Governance Is Emerging Above the Protocol Layer


**Suggested title:** *The Missing Layer Above MCP*

**Summary:**  
Recent research argues that interoperability standards such as MCP, A2A, and ACP enable communication, but not governance. Concepts like voting, dissent preservation, human escalation, and organisational decision-making require an architectural layer above today's protocols. 

**Angle:**  
This is a systems-thinking piece: protocols solve connectivity; organisations still need governance.

**Matches your interests:** Agent architecture, AI governance, distributed systems

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points**
- Coordination versus governance
- Human escalation
- Organisational control planes
- Multi-agent policy
- Future protocol evolution
