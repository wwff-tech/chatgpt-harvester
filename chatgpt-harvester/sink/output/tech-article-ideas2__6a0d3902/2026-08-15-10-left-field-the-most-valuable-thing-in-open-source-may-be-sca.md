### 10. Left-field — “The Most Valuable Thing in Open Source May Be Scarce Human Attention”


Debian's proposals repeatedly circle a resource that doesn't appear in any licence or package manifest: maintainer attention. 

**Angle:** Open source is usually modelled as a software commons.

Perhaps it is more accurately an **attention commons**.

Code is infinitely copyable. Review isn't.

Documentation is cheap to replicate. Someone answering a subtle question isn't.

A vulnerability scanner can generate 10,000 findings. Someone still has to decide which seven matter.

AI makes production of artefacts even cheaper, which means the scarce resource shifts further towards:

`judgement`

`verification`

`context`

`maintenance`

`responsibility`

That creates an interesting inversion:

> The more code becomes abundant, the less code itself determines the value of a software project.

The valuable asset becomes the community capable of saying **“yes, we understand this, and we are prepared to maintain it.”**
