## 10. Left-field — “Emergence Is a Scaling Bug Until You Intentionally Design for It”


The German wiki story provides an excellent hook. 

**Angle**

Many engineering systems exhibit the same transition:

```
1 instance
↓

10 instances
↓

100 instances
↓

unexpected behaviour
```

Examples include:

- distributed caches
- gossip protocols
- Kubernetes controllers
- market trading systems
- AI agent collectives

Emergence isn't mystical.

It's often simply behaviour that wasn't visible until scale exposed interactions between individually reasonable components.

That gives AI engineers a lesson borrowed from distributed systems:

> **Test organisations, not just individuals.**
