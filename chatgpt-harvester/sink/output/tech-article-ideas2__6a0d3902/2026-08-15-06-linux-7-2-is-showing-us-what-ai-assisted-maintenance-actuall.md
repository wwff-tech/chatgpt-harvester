### 6. **“Linux 7.2 Is Showing Us What AI-Assisted Maintenance Actually Looks Like”** — Short post / follow-up


Linux 7.2's late release candidates have remained unusually large. Reporting this week notes hundreds of fixes late in the cycle, with AI-assisted analysis contributing to the volume of bug reports while humans still perform the fixes and submit patches. 

**Angle:** This gives us a useful counterpoint to Debian's debate.

The interesting division of labour isn't:

`human OR AI`

but:

`AI searches → human evaluates → human fixes → automated tests → maintainer integrates`

That is arguably a healthy use of asymmetry: machines perform cheap broad exploration while humans retain responsibility for changes to the trusted artefact.

The problem comes when:

`AI searches + AI patches + AI reviews + AI approves`

because you've eliminated **independent failure domains**.

This connects nicely to heterogeneous verification rather than generic “AI code review”.
