### 9. Left-field — “Your Datacentre Has Become a Political Object”


Today's UK reporting says Police Scotland has advised robust physical security around a proposed AI datacentre in Larbert because of expected protests and possible disruption. Nearly 7,000 objections have reportedly been filed; Scotland currently has at least 23 hyperscale developments in planning, whose combined proposed demand would exceed Scotland's peak electricity use by more than 1.5×. 

**Angle:** This is not really an AI article. It's about infrastructure becoming **socially legible**.

People rarely protest a Kubernetes cluster.

They can protest:

`a 200 MW building`

`a substation`

`200 diesel generators`

`a new transmission line`

`a water abstraction licence`

AI is making computing physically visible again.

That means datacentre SRE increasingly intersects with physical security, grid engineering, planning law, environmental constraints, community relations, and geopolitical risk.

Cloud computing spent fifteen years persuading developers that infrastructure was abstract. AI may spend the next decade reminding everyone that **the cloud is an industrial facility**.
