## 5) SOHO Devices Are Accumulating Kernel Debt Through Their Supply Chains


**Suggested title:** *Your Router Inherited Someone Else's Technical Debt*

**Summary:**  
New research shows many routers and IoT devices remain locked to end-of-life Linux kernels because SoC vendors ship outdated SDKs that cascade through ODMs and OEMs. The result is inherited vulnerability debt throughout the supply chain. 

**Angle:**  
Frame this as a supply-chain governance problem rather than simply "vendors don't patch."

**Matches your interests:** Linux, embedded systems, supply-chain security

**Format:** **Long-form article**

**Suggested points**

- SoC SDK lock-in
- Firmware supply chains
- Regulatory pressure
- Community-maintained kernels
- Lessons for embedded product design
