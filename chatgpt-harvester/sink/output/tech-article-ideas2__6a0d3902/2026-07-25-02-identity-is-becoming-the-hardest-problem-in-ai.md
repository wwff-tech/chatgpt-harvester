## 2) Identity Is Becoming the Hardest Problem in AI


**Suggested title:** *AI Agents Need Access—Not Secrets*

**Summary:**  
Enterprise identity discussions are increasingly focused on AI agents acting as digital workers. The emerging consensus is that agents should use just-in-time, policy-governed access rather than holding long-lived credentials, with full ownership and audit trails. 

**Angle:**  
Frame this as the evolution of workload identity: service accounts → workload identities → agent identities.

**Matches your interests:** IAM, security, cloud, platform engineering

**Format:** **Long-form**

**Suggested points**

- JIT credentials
- Secretless architectures
- Ownership models
- Agent lifecycle management
- Auditability
