### 2. **“Generated-By Might Become the New Signed-off-by”** — Short post / technical essay


One Debian ballot option explicitly suggests commit trailers such as `Generated-By:` or `Assisted-By:` when a significant portion of a contribution was generated or substantially assisted by AI. 

**Angle:** This is more interesting than a badge saying “AI was used”.

Git already carries structured provenance:

`Signed-off-by:`

`Co-authored-by:`

`Reviewed-by:`

`Tested-by:`

An AI-assistance trailer could describe another dimension of the production process — but only if it remains useful rather than becoming performative compliance.

I'd push the idea further:

`Assisted-By: model/tool`

`Verified-By: human`

`Tested-By: CI evidence`

The key distinction is between **provenance and assurance**. Knowing a model generated something tells you almost nothing about whether it is correct; knowing who accepted responsibility for verifying it potentially tells you considerably more.
