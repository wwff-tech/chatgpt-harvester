## 2) IAM Policy Evaluation Complexity


- **Title:** *No One Knows What Your IAM Policies Actually Do*  
- **Summary:** Modern IAM systems (especially in cloud platforms) have reached a level of complexity where effective permissions are non-trivial to reason about.  
- **Angle:** “Access control as an undecidable problem (in practice)” — highlight emergent complexity.  
- **Matches Interests:** IAM, cloud security, systems modelling  
- **Format:** **Long form**

- **Suggested Points to Cover:**
  - Policy layering: roles, groups, conditions, deny rules  
  - Conflicting policies and evaluation order  
  - Debugging “why was access granted/denied?”  
  - Tooling gaps in visualising effective permissions  
  - Approaches: simplification, policy linting, simulation
