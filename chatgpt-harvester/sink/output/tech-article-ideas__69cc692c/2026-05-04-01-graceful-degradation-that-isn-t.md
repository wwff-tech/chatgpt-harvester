## 1) Graceful Degradation That Isn’t


- **Title:** *Your Fallback Path is a Hidden Single Point of Failure*  
- **Summary:** Systems designed with fallback mechanisms often rely on secondary paths that are less tested and less scalable.  
- **Angle:** “Resilience theatre” — fallback paths that fail under real load.  
- **Matches Interests:** SRE, distributed systems, reliability engineering  
- **Format:** **Long form**

- **Suggested Points to Cover:**
  - Common fallback patterns (read replicas, static responses, queues)  
  - Lack of parity in testing and capacity  
  - Failure amplification when fallback activates  
  - Observability of degraded modes  
  - Designing truly resilient fallback systems
