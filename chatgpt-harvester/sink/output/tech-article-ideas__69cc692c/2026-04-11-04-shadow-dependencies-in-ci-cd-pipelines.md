## 4) Shadow Dependencies in CI/CD Pipelines


- **Title:** *Your Build Depends on More Than You Think*  
- **Summary:** CI/CD pipelines often rely on implicit external services (package registries, APIs, mirrors) that introduce fragility.  
- **Angle:** “Pipelines as distributed systems” — treat builds as production workloads.  
- **Matches Interests:** DevOps, reliability, supply chain  
- **Format:** **Long form**

- **Suggested Points to Cover:**
  - Hidden dependencies in builds (network, mirrors, SaaS)  
  - Failure scenarios: outages, rate limits, tampering  
  - Reproducibility vs availability trade-offs  
  - Caching and vendoring strategies  
  - Hardening pipelines against external failure
